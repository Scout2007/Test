# *Operation Tidebreak*: the pre-release mini-series

**Status:** a draft plan for the user's review. **Sources:** the lore posts, `doctrine/LREF_doctrine.md` and `working_numbers.md`, `storyboard/tidebreak_data.py`, `ASSET_REQUESTS.md`, `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, `pack/project_docs/`.
**Tags:** [P1 §n] is point n of JCB's Part 1 post and [P1 classes] its class list; [P2] and [P3] are the frigate and destroyer posts; [Model] is the Blender README; A-nn, [LREF §n] and [WN §n] are the doctrine's assumptions, sections and working numbers; [User] marks a decision already made.

## 1. Concept

### Title options
1. ***Spin-Up*** (recommended). Everything in the film spins up before it acts (the warp rings, the CIWS barrels, the M-1C's wake), and the series is the film's spin-up.
2. ***Before Tidebreak***. Plain: a stranger knows what it is.
3. ***Hard Coat***. From JCB's "a soft sci fi setting wearing a hard sci fi coat".
4. ***Shields Off***. The order that opens every LREF battle [P1 §10].

### In-world framing
Each episode is in-world media from the months before the operation, made by whoever really would make it: the LREF and its fleet units, GUN-EWCC [P3], two contractors, a shipyard and two broadcasters. Eight are peacetime pieces that can't know Tidebreak is coming, which is the spoiler guard. The ninth is the news of the task group leaving.

### Series rules
- **Sound.** Narration, music and graphics belong to the medium. Footage of vacuum is silent unless the camera rides a hull (sound through structure) or the sound comes on an instrument channel: comms, telemetry, a contact mic.
- **Numbers.** Only the lore's and the doctrine's, with their limits. Assumptions put on screen (A-20, A-22 to A-25) become canon, so each episode lists them.
- **Look [User].** Beams are invisible and lenses glow violet only while firing. Blue appears only in the M-1C's bore pulse, heat only on the fins' radiator faces. In warp, fins are stowed and shields on.
- **No spoilers.** No Maren, Breakwater, Compact, Skerry, Breakers or Anchor, and nothing from the dropped AI plot line. No task-group names except the Astrid and the Endeavor; nothing damaged; no film framing copied. Systems appear in tests, drills and diagrams, never in battle. The series does teach the film's rules (time of flight, invisible beams, kill clouds, field reach, standoff, heat), so the film has less to explain.
- **No people on screen.** There are no character assets, so presenters are voices.
- **Assets.** New 3D work is film assets pulled forward, plus four cheap series-only set-ups: a witness plate, a one-mount laser and CIWS stage, clay and line overrides, and the missile's exploded split. Everything else is 2D, and every episode runs at 24 fps.

### What counts as "main"

| Light cruiser, battleship, carrier, dreadnought, chem-rails | Dropped | Not in the task group; chem-rails are "near to obsolete" [P1 §3] |

### Tag and end card
- **Tag (5 s, every episode).** A hard cut to the film's still star plate, made for its report cards. OPERATION TIDEBREAK types on in the report's white monospace; beneath it a mission-clock counter rolls from T−[n+1] to T−[n] (the film's "digits roll" rule), then the release date. The only sound is the film's prologue tone.
- **Finale.** The counter reaches T−0, and the card becomes the film's first card, *The record* (UNITED NATIONS ARMED FORCES · LONG RANGE EXPEDITIONARY FORCES / AFTER ACTION REPORT · OPERATION TIDEBREAK / RESTRICTED), which has no AI text.
- **End card** (the platform's end screen, outside the runtime): the next episode and its date, the playlist, and "Based on JCB's *Wearing Power Armor to a Magic School*. Ship designs by Sir_Lazz."

### Order and cadence
- **Order:** the guns, then the defences and missiles, then the ships up to the flagship, then the departure, with the tone alternating: raw, glossy, vintage, silent, comic, loud, uncanny, grave, live. It follows the film's build order: Eps 1–4 need built assets, the Q15 picks and step 1's first-version effects; Eps 5–9 need step 3–4 assets.
- **Cadence:** two a week for four and a half weeks, with the finale on release day as the film's pre-show. Finish Eps 1–4 before Ep 1 airs. If the series must run alongside the film's production, go weekly.

### Runtime and render
- **Runtime:** 9:26 over nine episodes of 41–90 s (13,584 frames).
- **Method:** the film's classes (A 45, A2 90, B 75, C 200, S 30, D 15 and E 2 s/frame at 1920×804), scaled by each style's pixel count. A one-mount stage ("ST") is costed from the measured M-1C stage (3–3.5 s/frame at 60 % and 64 spp): ~9 s/frame at full size, ~18 s as a macro at 128 spp.
- **Total: ~51 h raw, ~66 h with the film's 30 % re-render allowance**, about 40 % of a film pass. Keep it out of the film's pass week: the film's gate has under an hour to spare (Q17).

## 3. Episodes

### Ep 1 · *Acceptance Trial, Run 4* (M-1C, weapons-test footage, 60 s)
**Premise.** Declassified footage: an M-1C on a test stand wakes, fires each gun at a witness plate 100 km downrange, and is held for heat.

**Style**
- *Frame:* 4:3, often a 2×2 split of locked cameras, each with an ID and timecode.
- *Grain, colour:* sensor noise, flat and clipped; sunlit, where the film's *Fire* is dark; the high-speed window in monochrome.
- *Type:* a monospace data block (SLUG 10.0 KG · V₀ 25.0 KM/S · RANGE 100 KM · TOF), a slate, a red HOLD.
- *Camera:* locked cameras, a long-lens tracker, a replay marked HS 1,000 FPS.
- *Sound:* no music; range-control comms, countdown tones, and a contact mic on the mount.
- *Voice:* range control only: "Range is clear. Mount, you're hot."

**Beats**
1. 0–4 s: The slate.
2. 4–16 s: In 2×2: lids open on amber cells, louvres ripple, beacons flash; the clamps swing off; the cradle lays; the shell opens in your chosen wake style.
3. 16–21 s: "Gun A, on the tone." The 1.2 s charge, then in real time one white frame (the bore transit takes ~8 ms), the blast and the 7 m recoil; A's fins burst out, glowing.
4. 21–27 s: The replay: the blue pulse runs up the bore in the rig's 8 frames, which is 8 ms at 1,000 fps. Freeze at muzzle exit.
5. 27–33 s: The tracker: TOF counts to 4.0 s, then a white flash (3.1 GJ).
6. 33–38 s: Gun B fires; only B vents.
7. 38–50 s: HOLD. "Mount, hold for heat." The fins cool as the timecode jumps. SUSTAINED: 1 RD/GUN/~15 S.
8. 50–60 s: END OF RUN; the tag.

**Lore.** Kinetics, "magnetically accelerated" [P1 §3]; two guns per turret, two rails per gun [User]; 10 kg at 25 km/s, 3.1 GJ (A-20); 4.0 s to 100 km [WN §2]; one shot per gun every ~15 s sustained, because of heat [LREF §4.1]. The lore's "nearly 100 meter long railguns" [P1 §6] stay off screen, so nobody measures the model against them.

**Assets.** Reused: `LREF_Railcannon_M1C.blend` (`RC_M1C`, `RC_FireCycle`, the Stage and its `CAM_RC_*` cameras, the `tracer` blast, every control). New: a witness plate (series-only, built with `plating.py`); a comp flash until FX-SLUG exists; 2D overlays.

**Render.** ST windows 0.7 h, ST full frame 1.5 h, D 0.6 h, E 0.2 h: **~3 h**.

**Risks.** It needs the wake-style pick (Q15). The real-time window keys `shot_a` 0→0.33 in one frame; the replay plays the rig unchanged and stops at muzzle exit. That makes Q12's 0.33 s licence honest.

### Ep 2 · *Unseen* (laser focusing array, promotional advertisement, 42 s)
**Premise.** The array maker's brand film, shot like a luxury-watch ad: quilted beta cloth, a polished lens, a violet pulse, and nothing else to see.

**Style**
- *Frame:* 16:9, full bleed, no burn-ins.
- *Grain, colour:* clean, deep black; a hard sun key and a rim; saturated violet (glow kept at ~1–2 under AgX).
- *Type:* a thin, wide-tracked sans, one line per card, a logo.
- *Camera:* motion-control macro slides, rack focus, one slow orbit.
- *Sound:* a sparse pulse locked to the yoke servo's tick, heard through the hull as in the film's *Lenses*.
- *Voice:* close-miked and unhurried; three lines.

**Beats**
1. 0–6 s: Macro over tufted beta cloth and a strap. "Some things are made to be seen."
2. 6–12 s: Rack focus to the lens lip and its iris ring. "This one isn't."
3. 12–18 s: The gear sector turns as the yoke slews. Tick. Tick.
4. 18–25 s: The lens pulses violet; far behind, defocused, a spark winks out. NO BEAM. ONLY THE LENS.
5. 25–30 s: 3.3 m · 350 nm · 25 MW.
6. 30–35 s: Pull back to the array on the Endeavor's hull, a black PD laser beside it. "It won't sink a ship. It takes its eyes, its radiators and its missiles."
7. 35–42 s: The logo; the tag.

**Lore.** DEWs are "the namestays" [P1 §4]; the Endeavor's 8 arrays and 8 PD lasers [P1 §6, Model]; the invisible beam and violet lens [User]; 3.3 m, 350 nm, 25 MW (A-22); lasers "strip; they don't sink" [LREF §4.2].

**Assets.** Reused: the Endeavor's arrays (`laser_power`, `laser_traverse`, `laser_elevation`) and its `CAM_LookDev_*` cameras. New: a one-mount laser stage like the M-1C's (series-only, reused in Ep 3); FX-PD's intercept flash as the spark; 2D cards.

**Render.** Macro stage at 1080p 4.8 h, class A pull-back 2.0 h, E 0.1 h: **~7 h**.

**Risks.** Push the glow and AgX turns it lavender. An ad wants a visible beam, and it mustn't get one.

### Ep 3 · *Six Layers* (point defence, vintage training film, 75 s)
**Premise.** An old fleet training film, "revised edition: current fleet footage", in which a cheerful narrator walks new crews through the six layers between a ship and a missile.

**Style**
- *Frame:* 1.37:1, pillarboxed, with a leader countdown and splice flashes.
- *Grain, colour:* black and white, new footage graded to match; heavy 16 mm grain, weave and scratches, which also hide first-version effects.
- *Type:* hand-lettered titles and intertitles; cel-animated diagrams.
- *Camera:* locked tripod shots, slow zooms.
- *Sound:* mono with optical hiss; a library orchestra with a sting per layer. Hull shots carry the CIWS whine; drone shots are silent.
- *Voice:* a warm, confident mid-century narrator.

**Beats**
1. 0–6 s: The leader; SIX LAYERS · A FLEET TRAINING FILM.
2. 6–14 s: Six rings round a ship and a missile coming in from the edge. "Each layer gets what the last one missed."
3. 14–22 s: Layers 1–3: a jammer swings out, a drawn decoy, the arrays' ring at 30,000 km. "Blind it. Fool it. Burn it."
4. 22–30 s: Layer 4, a PD laser beside a main array, "from 5,000 kilometres in."
5. 30–40 s: Layer 5: chaff, flares and smoke bloom and thin. "No air holds a screen up. Fire it just in time."
6. 40–54 s: Layer 6: a CIWS side-on in its sponson, barrels blurring, and a cloud laid on the missile's path 5–30 km out. "The missile's own speed does the killing."
7. 54–66 s: A Casaba's jet reaching a hull from 2–4 km, and a red line at 10 km. "Anything that might be a Casaba dies before ten kilometres."
8. 66–75 s: THE END; the tag.

**Lore.** "Lasers, classic bulletstorm CIWS', kinetic kill clouds" and the hedgehog's "4 CIWS mounts" [P2]; the Endeavor's 16 CIWS, 8 PD lasers, 6 launchers and 2 jammers [Model]; the layers and the Casaba rule [LREF §6]; ~280 rounds/s and clouds at 5–30 km [WN §8]; powered flares and fast-thinning screens (A-27, A-28).

**Assets.** Reused: the Endeavor after the PDC pick, with EN-PD's `ciws_phase`, `ciws_fire`, `cm_chaff/flare/smoke` and `ew_active`; Ep 2's stage plus a CIWS mount; FX-PD and FX-SMOKE, whose step 1 versions pass under the grain. New: a vintage 2D kit.

**Render.** A2 smoke shot 3.7 h, A jammer 0.9 h, stage 0.7 h, E 0.7 h: **~6 h**.

**Risks.** The CIWS waits on the PDC pick (Q15); if that slips, swap Eps 3 and 4. The Casaba rule sets up the film's climax, which is fine as long as no target looks like a monitor.

### Ep 4 · *Field Reach* (plasma lance, technical-manual animation, 41 s)
**Premise.** An animated page of the Ryland-class manual: how a lance works, and why it stops where the ship's field stops.

**Style**
- *Frame:* 16:9 inside a manual page, with a header and page and FIG. numbers.
- *Grain, colour:* none: white paper, black lines, white clay, one violet-white accent.
- *Type:* a condensed technical sans, numbered callouts, a CAUTION box.
- *Camera:* orthographic and exploded views, slow turntables.
- *Sound:* a click per step and a tone on CAUTION; no music.
- *Voice:* none; the captions narrate.

**Beats**
1. 0–4 s: FIG. 1: the bow in clay ortho, its four lance slots ringed.
2. 4–10 s: A block diagram of a field projector and a plasma source: boxes, not a design.
3. 10–17 s: STEP 1, FIELD: a dashed sheath runs from the nose to the target. STEP 2, PLASMA: a jet runs down it. ~1,000 KM/S · ~1 GJ A PULSE.
4. 17–24 s: FIELD REACH ~50 KM: the sheath ends, and past it the jet blooms and fizzles.
5. 24–30 s: AIMING: the lances fire along the bow, so the hull turns to bear.
6. 30–34 s: FIG. 2: a torpedo that carries its own field generator.
7. 34–41 s: CAUTION: NOT FOR USE BEYOND FIELD REACH; the tag.

**Lore.** Plasma needs "an active electromagnetic field to maintain its form lest it just fizzles out": a torpedo carries its own field, a lance uses the ship's. It is "finicky and specialized" [P1 §4]. Four nose lances [Model]; ~1,000 km/s, ~1 GJ, ~50 km (A-23); a knife weapon [LREF §4.3].

**Assets.** Reused: the Endeavor's nose and `lance_power`. New: a clay and Freestyle line set-up (series-only); a stylised jet instead of FX-LANCE, which stays unseen for the film; 2D diagrams.

**Render.** Clay ortho at 1080p (class D) 2.3 h, E 0.3 h: **~2.6 h**.

**Risks.** A detailed cutaway would be a new weapon design and need concept sheets [User], so it stays boxes. The manual states A-23, the doctrine's reading of a soft-SF weapon, as fact.

### Ep 5 · *The Multitool* (Garibaldi-Ivanova frigate, infomercial, 90 s)
**Premise.** The shipyard's late-night infomercial. The pitch is JCB's frigate post nearly word for word, and its caveats become the fine print.

**Style**
- *Frame:* 4:3 SD video (rendered at 960×720), star wipes, an offer bar.
- *Grain, colour:* soft, over-saturated video with chroma bleed; the yard lit in loud colours by floodlight drones.
- *Type:* chrome bevelled words, starbursts, a CALL YOUR FLEET TENDER box, a tiny crawl.
- *Camera:* fast zooms, whip pans, before-and-after splits.
- *Sound:* a cheesy synth bed, studio-audience "ooh"s and a BUT WAIT stinger. The launches are silent (a drone camera).
- *Voice:* a loud pitchman, and a co-host feeding him problems.

**Beats**
1. 0–6 s: TIRED OF ONE-TRICK WARSHIPS? "Bought a battleship for a drone war?"
2. 6–18 s: The frigate turns. "One hull. Every job." Callouts: the shield, the warp rings, the hab section, radiators that retract for warp.
3. 18–28 s: The stripped hull ("like a fluffy cat out of a bath") beside the hedgehog. "Just shove in the hot-swappable missile pod module!"
4. 28–33 s: A drone along the hull as pod doors ripple open and plumes stream away. "About 360 pods!"
5. 33–53 s: "Need help at medium range?" Four kinetic batteries. "Intel says they've specced everything into drones?" Lasers, bulletstorm CIWS, kill clouds. Both modules appear as concept-sheet cards.
6. 53–61 s: "Just swap it out at your nearest fleet tender!" A tender cartoon.
7. 61–74 s: BUT WAIT: "the biggest, baddest, meanest dreadnought" against 5, 10 and 20 packs. "Does it really add up?"
8. 74–85 s: The crawl: "A sole MAV frigate is more than likely insufficient… Once expended, retains 4 CIWS mounts. Swaps require a fleet tender. Not a hero ship."
9. 85–90 s: The tag.

**Lore.** All [P2]: "the premier generalist platform"; ~400 m; ~360 pods (capital-ship killers in single pods, the back third in multi-silo packs, MIRVs); four batteries "filling in that awkward middle-range gap"; the PD fit; the tender; packs and "the economics of war"; the 4-CIWS limit; the cat. Plus ~700 missiles a hedgehog (A-26).

**Assets.** Reused: GI and GI-MAV with `pod_ripple` (film step 3); FX-SWARM with MSL-V, or the scaled MSL stand-in, enough at SD. New: the 2D kit; cards of the gun module, the PD module and the tender from their concept sheets (silhouettes in the film, Q13); a dreadnought silhouette.

**Render.** B at SD 4.9 h, the C launch 3.0 h, E 0.8 h: **~9 h**.

**Risks.** Comedy against a grim film, but the voice is JCB's own. Use a hull number, not the pack's names.

### Ep 6 · *Standoff* (nuclear warheads, trade-show reel, 55 s)
**Premise.** The loop a missile maker plays on its expo stand: the capital-ship killer and its Casaba warhead.

**Style**
- *Frame:* 16:9 for a video wall, with big centre-safe type and the maker's bar.
- *Grain, colour:* clean CG, navy gradients, cyan lines; the missile photoreal on black; SIMULATION on every engagement graphic.
- *Type:* a bold geometric sans, kinetic type, big numbers.
- *Camera:* CG orbits, smash zooms, speed ramps.
- *Sound:* driving electronic music with a hit on every word. It loops.
- *Voice:* none, since the expo floor would drown it.

**Beats**
1. 0–4 s: The logo; the nose turns out of black.
2. 4–14 s: Exploded turntable: the ablative nose, the seeker shutter opening, the warhead, the MIRV bus, the motor. HARDENED NOSE · SPIN · THREE GUIDANCE PATHS.
3. 14–20 s: 30 G · Δv 150 KM/S · 100,000 KM IN 18 MIN.
4. 20–28 s: NO BLAST WAVE IN VACUUM: a bare burst strips fins and sensors out to 1–3 km, but breaches a hull only within ~30–110 m.
5. 28–38 s: SIMULATION, on a box hull: a Casaba jet breaches the belt from 2–4 km. STANDOFF 2–4 KM.
6. 38–44 s: The missile slides into a hedgehog pod.
7. 44–55 s: The logo, a stand number, the loop point; the tag.

**Lore.** Nuclear means "Casaba howitzers, conventional, etc", "much more commonplace and reliable than even plasma" [P1 §3–4]; missiles are platforms [P1 §5]; capital-ship killers, MIRVs, "teller-device armed missiles" [P2]; vacuum effects [LREF §4.4, WN §7]; the Casaba jet (A-24); the missile's numbers and design (A-25).

**Assets.** Reused: MSL-V's capital-ship killer, whose LOD0 is built for the film's *Seeker* (`seeker_shutter`, `cap_glow`, spin); a GI-MAV pod. New: the exploded split, with sections set by the concept sheet; 2D graphics.

**Render.** The missile as a one-object stage at 1080p 1.6 h, E 0.5 h: **~2 h**.

**Risks.** It waits on the missile concept and MSL-V (film steps 2 and 4); if they're late, swap Eps 6 and 7. The real Casaba jets stay in the film.

### Ep 8 · *The Pocket Battleship* (Hanuman heavy cruiser and spinal cannon, documentary, 68 s)
**Premise.** A segment of a public documentary series: how the heavy cruiser went from glass cannon to pocket battleship, told through the Astrid.

- *Camera:* slow dollies, pans across drawings, one long-lens archive shot.
- *Sound:* strings and piano over silent footage; the score drops out for the spinal flash.
- *Voice:* an older historian, measured and dry.

**Beats**

### Ep 9 · *Undisclosed* (Ryland cruiser Endeavor, news segment, 75 s)
**Premise.** Evening news: an LREF task group, the Endeavor nearest the camera, leaves on a deployment the service won't describe. The live picture ends as the warp rings light, and the segment hands over to the film.

**Style**
- *Frame:* 16:9 broadcast, with a bug, a LIVE tag, a ticker and FILE tags.
- *Grain, colour:* sharpened, compressed video; macroblocking and pumping exposure on the long lens; no heat shimmer, since there's no air.
- *Type:* the network's lower thirds and ticker, with no headline about the group.
- *Camera:* a station's operator-driven long lens, overshooting each reframe, the ship under a third of the frame; closer FILE clips from a public flyby.
- *Sound:* the news theme, the studio and a link; the pictures are silent.
- *Voice:* a neutral anchor and a live, slightly breathless reporter.

**Beats**
1. 0–6 s: "An LREF task group is leaving tonight on a deployment the service won't describe."
2. 6–16 s: LIVE: the Endeavor over the planet, shield on, fins stowed, rings turning. L.R.E.F.S. ENDEAVOR · RYLAND-CLASS CRUISER.
3. 16–26 s: FILE: turrets on their travel locks, a laser array, the missile cells. "Every weapon family the fleet has, on one hull."
4. 26–34 s: LIVE: fourteen sets of running lights. "Behind her, the flagship Astrid. Fourteen ships in all."
5. 34–42 s: FILE: the gold shield. "It rides ahead of her in warp. Before a fight, they leave it with the tender."
6. 42–52 s: LIVE: the rings brighten as the clock jumps three minutes. "There's no sound out here. Just the light."
7. 52–58 s: A blue-white bloom whites out the camera, which recovers on empty stars. "And she's gone."
8. 58–64 s: "The LREF says it will release details when it can." Freeze; the signal drops.
9. 64–75 s: Black; the counter rolls to T−0; the film's first card and the release date.

**Lore.** [P1 §6]: the Ryland class, an exploratory variant of the Grace class, with railguns, arrays, lances and missiles but no spinal cannon.
