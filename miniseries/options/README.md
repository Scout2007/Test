## Notes on the picks

- **Two changes from the plan.** Ep 6 moves from the trade-show reel to the hearing (cheaper, and it teaches standoff by cross-examination), and Ep 8 from the documentary to an oral history (the spinal's patience in a human voice; the firing is never shown). If you'd rather keep the planned styles there, A is fully worked out in both.
- **Overlaps to watch.** Ep 1 B and Ep 6 B are both test-range films; Ep 3 B, Ep 8 B and Ep 9 are all broadcasts. Choosing either B alongside its neighbour makes the run feel repetitive.
- **Cheap swaps.** Ep 7 B (~1.7 h) and Ep 3 C (~6.1 h, safest on the CIWS pick, Q15) cost least if render time is tight.
- **Canon.** Several options show derived numbers (Ep 3's time-to-hull clock, Ep 4's ~2 g of plasma, Ep 6's ~700 missiles per hedgehog). Each file's "Lore facts shown" and "Risks" list what would become canon.
- **Placeholders.** Makers, yards, hull numbers and presenters stay in [brackets] until you name them (plan question 7).
