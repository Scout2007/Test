# Ep 4 · Plasma lance (T−6)

Three separate events, one idea each. *Field Reach* is a silent publication plate: the lance's barrel is a field that has to be built first, and then the pulse goes down it. *Sinkex* is a range exercise heard on an observer loop: a retired crew member watches the Endeavor cut her old ship in two and talks about a coat. *The Edge* is one chase shot beside a single pulse, ending when it comes apart. They share no speaker, line, gag or shot idea. The lance's reach appears only in C.

## Option A · *Field Reach*: publication plate, clay and line (45 s)

**Logline.** A maintenance publication's plate for the Endeavor's nose plays as a short 3D sequence: four rings are drawn, a sleeve grows out of them, a point of light is gathered and let go, and the sleeve rings once and fades.

**The idea.** A thrown thing needs a barrel, and here the barrel is built first out of nothing. The viewer should come away feeling that this is a fixed, orderly procedure that is done the same way every time. It never says why.

**Look and sound.**
- White paper, black Freestyle line, white clay material override on the real nose, and one violet-white accent used only for the sleeve lines and the point of light.
- Orthographic and slow three-quarter views. The camera never moves faster than a page turning.
- Captions are single labels: FIG. 4-1 BOW, 1 FIELD, 2 CHARGE, 3 RELEASE. Footer: [PUBLISHER] · [PUB. No.]. No numbers beyond the step and figure marks, and no sentences.
- Sound is a soft click for each ring and each step, a low rising hum under the sleeve, one flat knock at the release, then a page-turn rustle. No music and no voice.
- The lenses are never shown firing. The release is shown only by the point vanishing and the sleeve flickering.

## Option B · *Sinkex*: range observer loop (60 s)

**Logline.** A stripped, retired frigate is cut in two by the Endeavor while a retired crewman on the observer loop watches and talks about the coat they never got back.

**The idea.** A ship's end is a personal thing for someone. The weapon is only how it happens, so it is a few single frames and then silence. The viewer should feel the old person's dry restraint.

**Look and sound.**
- The camera is fixed on the tender's observation mast: long lens, flat sun, cold digital video grade, a small corner overlay (SINKEX · OBS-2 · [RANGE No.] · [HULL No.]). It looks over a pale, generic grey world.
- Vacuum is silent. The only sound is what the tender carries: its low hull hum, and the observer loop (hiss, squelch, voices). The loop is dead for the moments of the cut.
- Three voices: RANGE (a young officer, procedural), VETERAN (retired, dry, older) and WEAPONS on the Endeavor.
- The lance is seen from far away as one-frame streaks with nothing visible between ship and hull. The hit and the parting are the picture.
- No lens flash and no jet shown, as the lance's own look belongs to the other options.

## Option C · *The Edge*: one chase shot (43 s)

**Logline.** One camera drone rides beside a single pulse out from the Endeavor's nose, and the operator following it breathes through it until the pulse frays and goes out, far from the ship.

**The idea.** The lance is a thing that ends. There is no impact and no target: the picture is a long, held run along a thread, and the feeling is the quiet when it stops. Nobody says how far it was.

**Look and sound.**
- Unbroken: one camera path, no cuts. It starts a metre off the four lenses and slides out beside the pulse, then turns to look back at the end.
- Raw instrument feed: faint noise, slight vignetting, a small drone-feed frame (DRN-L [No.] · FWD · TRK) with a range counter whose digits blur and then freeze. This is the only place a number appears, and it can be dropped.
- Sound is the control loop: hiss, the lock tone, the drone's faint servo, and the operator's breathing. Calm at the start, held while the pulse runs, then let out. The one word is spoken after the pulse dies.
- The lock tone is the ruler: steady while the pulse is whole, wavering as it frays, a flat low note and then silence when it is gone.
- The lens flash is shown here, close, and nowhere else in the episode.

## Recommendation

Pick C: it is the one image nobody has seen, it fits the series' silent slot for this episode, and it has the least dialogue to defend. Take B only if you want a human voice this early; take A for the cheapest, but it is the closest to the rejected manual idea. Decide whether C keeps its drone-feed range counter, and whether the operator's one word stays.
