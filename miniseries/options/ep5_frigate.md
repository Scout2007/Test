# Ep 5 · Frigate and tender (T−5)

Three separate events about one hull. **A** is a late-night ad that sells the frigate's range of jobs while its fine print says the opposite. **B** is a night shift at the tender, seen through the loader arm's camera, in which a module swap goes unfinished at shift change. **C** is three sister frigates leaving formation on an open channel, teasing each other before they go to three jobs. A is a joke told to the audience, B is a wait watched in silence, and C is a family scene heard on a radio.

## Option A · *The Multitool*: late-night ad (50 s)

**Logline.** A pitchman sells the Garibaldi-Ivanova frigate as the warship that does every job, and the small print quietly corrects him.

**The idea.** The frigate can be anything, but only one thing at a time, and changing takes a while. The viewer learns it from a caption the pitchman never reads out.

**Look and sound.**
- Shopping-channel video, 4:3 at 960×720: soft, saturated, with chroma bleed and loud floodlit colours. Star wipes, one chrome title, an offer bar. One before-and-after split.
- The pitchman is loud and warm. The co-host is a real person who would rather be somewhere else.
- A cheesy synth bed, studio-audience "ooh" and clapping, and a BUT WAIT stinger. The demos play silent under the music, because the cameras are in vacuum.
- Captions carry the fine print. The pitchman never reads them.

## Option B · *Shift Change*: the tender's arm camera (55 s)

**Logline.** On the night shift at the tender, a frigate's module is swapped by a crew we only hear, and at dawn the job is handed on with the old module still hanging on the arm.

**The idea.** Behind every ship that sails is slow, dull, shared work. Nobody here is hurrying, and nothing about it is heroic.

**Look and sound.**
- One fixed camera on the loader arm, in 16:9 at 1280×720: a dim, work-lit grade, soft vignette, a plain clock overlay in the corner.
- Most of the film is a time-lapse on twos, with lamp light and sun racing across the hull. A few moments return to real time.
- The camera hears only through the arm: servo hum, grip clunks, the ticking of cooling metal. The crew are a radio loop that surfaces from the blur and drops back.
- Nobody explains the job. The lines are the kind two tired people say at four in the morning.

## Option C · *Sisters*: a shared channel (55 s)

**Logline.** Three frigates of one hull and three different modules hold formation until the clock runs out, tease each other on an open channel, and part for three jobs.

**The idea.** One hull, three characters. What each sister carries has made her who she is, and the one who stays behind says the kindest thing.

**Look and sound.**
- Scope 2.39:1 at 1920×800, clean and cool. A banded gas giant fills the lower frame. The sun comes from the left.
- Heard on a comm loop: carrier hiss, squelch clicks, the odd breath. Never from outside a hull.
- Subtitles in the film's comm style, with callsign tags: GUNNY, HEDGEHOG, PICKET.
- Slow, locked-off cameras that settle on one hull at a time. The plumes are quiet.

## Recommendation

Make **A**. It is the series' comic slot, it uses only the three module renders, and its joke lives in a caption, so nothing is explained. If the user wants the ep to carry the tender and hold a quiet beat, swap in **B**, which is the cheapest and has the fewest words. **C** is the most work and the least needed. The user decides whether the series wants a laugh here or a quiet shift, and whether an ad belongs in a grim film's pre-show.
