## Full report

### 1. The story as understood

A planet called Maren has broken away from "the GUN", which I take to be the power it belonged to; the film never says why. The GUN's Long Range Expeditionary Forces send a task group of fourteen warships to take Maren's sky so its troops, T-SEC, can land. The one rule: not one shot at the planet itself.

Maren's sky has three defences: a big warship, Breakwater (later captioned a monitor with no warp drive); lasers on the ground, chiefly Site One, the only one covering Breakwater's patch of sky; and the Breakers, a ring of wreckage hiding missiles, railguns, drones and a frigate. Maren's moon, Skerry, adds a long-range laser and a mass driver.

The task group has a kilometre-long flagship, Astrid, built around a huge "spinal" gun; the cruiser Endeavor, which the film mostly follows, with lasers, guns and a short-range plasma "lance"; a sensor destroyer, Canterbury; at least two missile frigates, Infinity and Galactica; Extenuating, which hijacks drones and steers the fleet's missiles; small ships (corvettes, I think); and several names I can't place.

They jump in 450,000 km out; the debris won't let them come closer. The ships detach gold "warp shields" and leave them with Nauvoo. Skerry throws three rounds down their path, due in six hours, and turns its laser on them. That laser can burn radiators from anywhere, so Infinity fires 150 missiles at the moon, and the laser dies. The ships put out their radiator fins, edge-on to the planet's lasers, and coast for two hours.

In the Breakers, Site One dazzles Endeavor's sensors, and missiles hidden in the rocks attack. A hidden railgun destroys Canterbury, with no survivors; Astrid's spinal gun destroys the railgun. Drones hatch from the rocks. Extenuating hijacks the networked ones, but an autonomous one destroys Normandy.

The fleet flips, brakes and hides behind a big rock, Anchor, with 18 km of rock between it and Site One. Endeavor is overheating and puts its fins out before the area is cleared. A second hidden railgun shoots one off. A Maren frigate slides out of a small moon and hits Endeavor, which destroys it with the lance, then kills the railgun with its turrets.

The plan:
- The missile frigates stay in Anchor's shadow and fire two waves.
- The "spend" wave makes Breakwater open fire and show her guns.
- The "kill" wave hides forty nuclear "spears" to wreck her drive.
- Everyone else stays out of her range.

Skerry's rounds arrive on time and are dodged. Breakwater fires 64 missiles at Extenuating; Astrid shoots them all down. Endeavor blinds Site One. The kill wave is steered round Breakwater's exposed guns, and the spears kill her engines.

Breakwater is ordered to surrender and gives no answer. Astrid fires, and 2 minutes 49 seconds later the slug breaks her back. Maren asks for terms: switch off Site One, and chart every mine in the Breakers. Site One goes dark. Endeavor, forced to dump its water to cool down, runs out its three surviving fins. Nauvoo is told to bring the shields in, and T-SEC that it's next.

It ends this way because Breakwater was Maren's main defence, and with her drive dead she couldn't dodge a slow but certain shot. The no-fire rule shaped everything else: Site One was hidden from, blinded and switched off through terms, never destroyed.
