Source: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Test/7165575b-ed81-59e3-b92a-a398fd9f61d5/scratchpad/coldread/r7/audience_script.md`. I read only this file. Where I'm inferring something, or using knowledge from outside the film, I say so.

## 1. The story as I understood it

**Who and why.** A fourteen-ship fleet, callsign Tidebreak, drops out of what I took to be a warp jump about 450,000 from a populated planet (I assume the unit is kilometres). The enemy is "Maren". I read that as the planet and its government, though the planet is never labelled. The film never says why the fleet attacks. Its only stated objective is the warship Breakwater.

**What each side has.** The fleet has:
- the flagship Astrid, which carries a spinal gun;
- the cruiser Endeavor, our point-of-view ship, with lasers, gun turrets and a plasma "lance";
- two electronic-warfare destroyers, Canterbury and Extenuating;
- two missile frigates, Infinity and (I infer) Galactica;
- a gun frigate, Donnager;
- three corvettes: Wallfish, Normandy and one unnamed;
- the tender Nauvoo.

The ships arrive with gold "shields" across their bows. They detach them to uncover their guns and leave them with Nauvoo, and three ships stay behind. Every ship sheds heat through fold-out radiator fins, and the fins are fragile.

Maren has Breakwater, a "compact monitor" with no warp drive. It is parked over the planet, under the cover of Site One, a laser on the ground. Maren also has Skerry, a moon with a launcher and a laser, and the Breakers, a ring of rocks seeded with hidden missiles, guns and drones. The fleet's rules forbid firing on the planet's surface.

**What happens.**
- **Skerry.** Minutes after arrival, Skerry fires three rounds into the fleet's path, due in six and a half hours, and lases Astrid's dish. Actual, the commander, has Skerry killed first because its laser burns radiators. Infinity's missiles destroy the laser and the launcher, but more rounds are already in flight.
- **The run in.** Eleven ships burn toward the planet, then coast with radiators out.
- **The Breakers.** The radiators go back in. Site One dazzles the fleet's sensors, missiles burst out of the rocks, and a buried railgun destroys Canterbury, apparently with no survivors. Astrid's spinal gun wrecks the railgun. Drone swarms launch. Extenuating hijacks the linked drones and turns them on the rest, but an autonomous one kills Normandy.
- **Anchor.** The fleet flips, brakes and hides in the shadow of a rock called Anchor, out of Site One's sight. Endeavor is at 94% heat and runs out its radiators before the area is cleared. A gun on another rock shatters one fin, and a frigate hidden in a moonlet hits the hull. Endeavor lances the frigate, silences the gun with its turrets and cools off.
- **The plan.** The missile frigates fire from Anchor's shadow, guarded by Donnager and Wallfish. Everyone else follows Actual toward a firing point marked for Astrid. Salvo one is decoys to draw Breakwater's point defence. Salvo two, forty "Casabas", is meant to kill her drive.
- **The attack.** Breakwater fires 64 missiles at Extenuating, which is guiding the salvos; Astrid covers it, and all 64 are shot down. Endeavor puts full laser power on Breakwater and only dazzles Site One. The decoys draw fire. The Casabas turn out to be nuclear jets, and they kill her engines.
- **The kill.** Actual demands surrender and gets no answer. Astrid fires, and 2:49 later the round breaks Breakwater's back as she comes into sunlight.

**How it ends.** Endeavor, at 98% heat, dumps boiling water. Maren asks for terms, and Actual demands that Site One go dark and that the mines be charted. Site One's lights go out, and Endeavor finally runs out its three surviving fins. Actual calls the shields forward and tells "T-SEC" it's next. I couldn't decode that line.

**Why it ends that way.** With Breakwater, Skerry and the rock defences gone, Maren has nothing left to fight with. The fleet uses the surrender to get rid of the one weapon its own rules never let it shoot.

## 3. Questions answered from the film alone

**a. Why does the fleet arrive so far away?**
Not sure. The film only tells me the distance was planned ("Four-fifty thousand, on the mark", 4). I have two guesses, both inference:
- It's a safe distance to take off the shields, which cover the guns and sensors (5), and to set up the sensor net (6) before closing in.
- By genre convention, warp can't drop a ship any nearer a planet.

The first guess is weakened by Skerry reaching them with a laser within four minutes (8).

**b. What are the fins or radiators for, and why do they matter?**
Fairly sure: they get rid of heat.
- **Heat:** the readouts climb while the fins are stowed (94% at 30, 88% at 51, 98% at 60) and fall while they're out (31% at 43). Even a turret pops glowing fins after firing (41).
- **Fragility:** they're what the enemy aims for. "Skerry's laser burns radiators; it dies first" (9), and a slug shatters Endeavor's port radiator (35).
- **Why it matters:** the fleet keeps trading cooling against safety. The fins go edge-on to the planet (14), in at the Breakers (15), and out only in Anchor's shadow (30–32 and 42). Boiling water is dumped when they can't come out (60). They come out for good only after Site One goes cold (63).

I'm not sure what happens at 100%. I assume the ship can't keep fighting.

**c. Why doesn't the fleet simply destroy the ground laser, Site One?**
The rules of engagement forbid it: "ROE: no fire on the surface" (9), and Site One is on the ground (30, 62). I'm sure of the rule, but not of why it exists. My guess is the populated planet (city lights at 48 and 63), or a wish for surrender rather than ruin. So the fleet:
- hides from it (30);
- smokes it (45);
- dazzles it without burning it (51);
- makes its shutdown a surrender term (61–62).

**d. What is the plan against Breakwater, step by step? Who stays where?**
1. Shelter in Anchor's lee, out of Site One's sight, vent heat and clear the nearby rocks (30–42).
2. The missile frigates (Infinity, and Galactica by inference) stay at Anchor and fire from its shadow. Donnager and Wallfish stay to guard them (43, 46–47).
3. "Rest, on me": Actual takes everyone else toward a firing point marked "ASTRID", short of Breakwater (43–45). That means Astrid, Endeavor, Extenuating and, I assume, the third corvette; a small warship is seen with them at 44.
4. Salvo one is decoys to draw Breakwater's point defence. Infinity launches it at T+7:06:57, on target at 7:52:00 (43, 46, 52).
5. Salvo two is forty Casabas to kill her drive. Galactica launches it at T+7:08:30, on target at 7:53:30 (43, 47, 54–55).
6. On the way in, Extenuating guides the missiles; I inferred that from "She's after our guidance". When Breakwater goes for Extenuating, Astrid covers it and becomes backup guidance (49–50). Endeavor lasers Breakwater and only dazzles Site One (51).
7. With the drive dead, Actual demands surrender, then Astrid's spinal gun finishes her (56–59). This step is my reading of the "ASTRID" marker; the spoken plan stops at "kills her drive".

**Who stays where:**
- Nauvoo and two others wait at the arrival point with the shields (5, 12).
- The missile frigates, Donnager and Wallfish stay at Anchor.
- Everyone else goes with Actual.

**e. Why does the flagship wait until the enemy can't move before firing its big gun?**
Inferred, but I'm fairly confident. The spinal round is slow: about 1:50 to the railgun rock (24–25) and 2:49 to Breakwater (58). A ship that can manoeuvre could get out of the way in that time; Canterbury is shown trying to dodge slugs (22). With her drive dead, Breakwater is a sitting target. The pause also leaves room for the surrender demand (56). The film never says any of this outright.

**f. Who was lost, and when?**
- **Canterbury** (e-war destroyer): T+3:52:13, hit by slugs from a buried railgun. "No beacons", so I assume no survivors (21–23).
- **Normandy** (corvette): T+4:25, killed by a drone that slipped through (28).
- Both are confirmed "NO CARRIER" at T+8:10 (61).
- **Endeavor** survives, but loses her port radiator at T+5:13:16 (35) and takes a hull hit at T+5:13:30 (36). I'm not sure about casualties aboard.
- **Enemy losses:**
  - Skerry's laser and driver at T+1:02;
  - the railgun rock at T+3:54:28;
  - the hidden frigate at T+5:13:55 (escape pods are seen);
  - the rock gun at T+5:14:40;
  - Breakwater at T+7:57:29. I'm not sure whether anyone got off her.

**g. How does it end?**
- Breakwater breaks apart (59), and Endeavor dumps water at 98% heat (60).
- Maren asks for terms, and Actual demands that Site One go dark and that the mines be charted (61).
- Site One's lights go out (62), and Endeavor runs out her three remaining fins (63).

Maren. I take that to be the planet and its government, but that's inference, and I'm not sure who they are or why they're the enemy. What they have:

## 4. Dialogue and on-screen text

**Does it sound like real radio?** Mostly yes, and it's the film's strongest feature:
- Calls open with callsigns: "Infinity, Actual. Engage Skerry."
- Numbers come in digit groups: "nine-four", "two forty-nine".
- The brevity codes are used correctly as far as I know, and the picture always explains them.
- Nearly every line reacts to something on screen instead of narrating.

If anything it's cleaner than real traffic: almost nobody acknowledges anything ("Copy" appears once, at 5). Every voice is a ship or "Actual", with no named people, so the emotional weight rests on ship names.

**Lines that worked**

## 5. Score

**7/10.** The battle's cause and effect holds together and the clock arithmetic checks out: heat against radiators, the ban on surface fire, decoys before Casabas, and killing the drive before the slow kill shot. But the film never says who the fleet is or why it's fighting, and it leans on unexplained jargon and a few loose threads at its most important moments.
