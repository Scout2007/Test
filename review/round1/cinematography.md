# Round 1 review: cinematography and editing

## Verdict

The four-act shape and the beat order are right, and several shots already sell scale honestly. As timed, though, the film can't be watched and read at once: text is on screen for about 80% of it, 31 of 37 shots last 3–5 s whatever they carry, and at the stated lenses most kills are a few pixels wide. To fix it:
- re-time to about 3:20;
- cut the subtitles hard;
- add long-lens coverage and a clock and HUD plan;
- set up the Canterbury, the Normandy and Breakwater before they matter.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MAJOR] Subtitles bury the picture (film-wide; worst are 28, 5, 32 and 36)
- **Problem:** 36 of 37 shots carry comm lines, 51 in all. At 15 cps, with a floor of 1 s per line, they need about 132 of the film's 165 s. The audience reads instead of watching, and the setup (5), the geography (28) and the resolution (36) can't be read at all.
- **Evidence:** Text-only reading speed per shot:

  | Shot | cps | Note |
  |---|---|---|
  | 28 | 29 | 117 characters in 4 s, on top of HUD labels |
  | 5 | 26 | |
  | 32 | 25 | |
  | 36 | 22 | |
  | 6, 20, 30 | 19 | |
  | 7, 26 | 16 | |
  | 4, 12, 13, 24, 31 | about 15–16 | |
  | 17 | — | three speakers and an internal cut in 5 s |

  Speaker tags add to the load: EXTENUATING CIRCUMSTANCES (25 characters) appears 6 times, TIDEBREAK ACTUAL 16 times.
- **Fix:** Don't subtitle what the picture already shows: drop 2's line and trim 11, 14, 21 and 25. Use short, dim tags: ACTUAL (TIDEBREAK ACTUAL · ASTRID on first use), EXTENUATING, ASTRID, ENDEAVOR. No line should cross a cut; if one must, hold it at least 12 frames past the cut. At the durations in #2, all of these rewrites read at 12 cps or less:
  - **5:** "Skerry's thrown three rounds down our lane." / "They'll be hours." Tag the HUD **ARRIVES T+6:40**. The sketch's "TOF 6 h 40 m" from T+0:04 lands at T+6:44, not at shot 30's T+6:40.
  - **6:** "Picture's up. Breakwater's right where the brief said."
  - **9:** "Splash on Skerry. Their laser's gone." Viewers will read "Two tracks down" as two of the three rounds.
  - **12:** "Site One's painting us! Shutters!"
  - **13:** "Launch, launch! Cold birds off the rocks!"
  - **20:** "One's through—on Normandy!" Move "Normandy's gone. Close the gap." to 21, whose own line becomes "Turnover. One at a time."
  - **26:** "Frigate off the rock, sixty k! Engaging."
  - **28:** "The Lee, Breakwater, Site One: one line. Spend wave on my mark." Cut the Excelsior line.
  - **29:** "Mark." / "Spend wave away." Cut the Infinity line.
  - **30:** "Skerry's rounds. Right on time." / "All ships, left two degrees."
  - **32:** "Their net's down." / "All arrays: dazzle Breakwater."
  - **33:** "Kill wave, on target in three, two—"
  - **36:** "Actual, Maren's asking for terms." / "Site One goes dark first." The film never introduces "MDC".

### 2. [MAJOR] One tempo throughout starves the key beats (runtime)
- **Problem:** 31 of 37 shots run 3–5 s (mean 4.5 s), whether they establish, kill or end the film. There is no slow opening, no fast ambush and no breath after a death.
- **Evidence:**
  - Shot 35 holds the slew, the shot, a three-minute flight, the impact, the break-up and two lines in 7 s.
  - The costly victory (36–37) gets 11 s.
  - The 2 h 10 min coast (10→11) is a plain cut.
  - The Canterbury breaks up, and the very next frame orders the Astrid to turn.
- **Fix:**
  - Re-time:

    | Shot | Now | Proposed | Why |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | 1 | 6 s | 8 s | The ship needs about 3 s after the bloom |
    | 3 | 5 s | 7 s | |
    | 5+6 | 7 s | 12 s | See #4 |
    | 10 | 4 s | 7 s | Play the coast on the clock |
    | 16 | 4 s | 5 s | Hold on the hiss |
    | 17 | 5 s | 8 s | |
    | 24 | 4 s | 6 s | |
    | 25 | 6 s | 9 s | |
    | 28 | 4 s | 7 s | |
    | 35 | 7 s | 10 s | |
    | 36 | 4 s | 7 s | |
    | 37 | 7 s | 12 s or more | |

  - Cut the ambush (13–16) faster: 2–3 s shots with ECU inserts (#10).
  - In 35, keep the flight. After "Impact in three minutes", hold on the crippled Breakwater, its turrets swinging uselessly, with the clock racing, then cut to the hit. That hold is the film's best suspense.
  - The total comes to about **3:20**, which the story needs. To stay under 3:00, fold 19 into 18 and 26 into 27, and drop the park business in 4.

### 3. [MAJOR] At real lenses the far end of each exchange is invisible (3, 15, 17, 24–27, 35)
- **Problem:** Kills happen at 40–15,000 km but are framed on 28–50 mm lenses set for the near ship.
- **Evidence (36 mm sensor):**
  - **27:** a 350 m frigate at 40 km is about 15 px wide at 28 mm, so its opening midsection and pods can't be seen.
  - **26:** from over the Donnager at 40 mm, the frigate is about 10 px.
  - **15:** the Canterbury, 600 km off at 50 mm, is about 1 px.
  - **3:** Skerry is about 57° from Maren, but the 85 mm frame is only 24° wide.
- **Fix:** Add a long-lens "tracker" camera: 600–2,000 mm, heavy slow pans, sensor noise.
  - **27:** a 2 s CU on the nose, then 1,000 mm on the frigate, which fills about a quarter of the frame as it breaks.
  - **26:** 600 mm from the Endeavor's hull. The frigate clears its rock and fires, and the hull camera shakes with the hit.
  - **Match the framing across each hit:**
    - 17's impact frames 15's rock.
    - 24 gets a half-second of the platform's muzzle flash, and 25's impact matches it.
    - 35's impact uses 34's angle, so we see the slug enter the Casaba breach.
  - **3:** drop Skerry; 5 introduces it.

### 4. [MAJOR] No clock or HUD plan, so the viewer loses place and time (Acts II–IV)
- **Problem:** 32 of 36 cuts jump time, but the board never says how the clock behaves. There are only three HUD inserts (5, 19, 28), each 3–4 s long. None comes at the ambush or at the Lee, where threats arrive from places never shown.
- **Evidence:**
  - The Astrid is 8,000 km back in 17, and that gap is never shown.
  - The platform's rock (800 km) and the frigate's rock (60 km) are never established.
  - The five largest jumps are all plain cuts: 8→9 (38 min), 10→11 (2 h 10 min), 21→22 (34 min), 29→30 (58 min) and 36→37 (1 h 25 min).
  - Shot 5, the Astrid's telescope feed, plays before 6 shows the Astrid's dish. We see what the eye sees before we see the eye.
- **Fix:**
  - **Clock:**
    - Make it small and persistent, in one corner, clear of the subtitles.
    - Start it on the flash in 1, at T+0:00:00.
    - Run it at the shot's rate: spinning seconds say "compressed" and crawling seconds say "slowed".
    - On jumps of 30 minutes or more, roll the digits for 12–18 frames or dissolve.
  - **HUD:** one master plot, with Maren screen right as in the live action.
    - Each insert runs at least 5 s and carries no more than 4 labels.
    - The clock and "ENDEAVOR SINK nn %" sit in a fixed place, so the heat clock builds toward shot 23.
  - **Use the HUD at:**
    - **6:** restructure as the dish (3 s), then 5's Skerry feed (4 s), then the master plot (5 s) showing Breakwater, Site 1, Skerry, the Breakers and the approach line.
    - **17:** the Astrid swings its bearing line onto the platform. This shows the slew honestly.
    - **22:** the Lee's shadow, with two nearby rocks tagged "?".
    - **28:** as now.

### 5. [MAJOR] The losses and the antagonist aren't set up (6, 11, 15–20, 29–37)
- **Problem:** The Canterbury is never seen or heard before it dies, and the Normandy never speaks. Breakwater first appears at 2:11 and is dead by 2:34. The three missile waves blur together.
- **Evidence:**
  - The hiss in 16 and "Remember the Cant." in 17 land on a ship with no face.
  - Viewers will read 29's launch as 33's arrival.
  - The spend wave's arrival at T+6:01, which explains why the kill wave gets through, is never shown.
  - Nothing in 36–37 recalls the lost ships.
- **Fix:**
  - **The Canterbury:** give it an early shot and line, for example its dish unfolding in 6 ("Net's up. Going quiet.").
  - **The Normandy:** give it 18's line.
  - **Breakwater:** show it in 6's plot as a telescope image over Maren's limb, so that 31 becomes a reveal.
  - **The waves:** say "spend wave" and "kill wave" in the lines, as the phase table already does. Add 3 s at T+6:01 of the spend wave dying as a flicker over the limb (class D).
  - **The losses:** add one callback in 36–37, such as two dead channels on a final plot.

### 6. [MAJOR] Time compression turns kilometre-long hulls into models (17, 24, 25, 35; 8, 21)
- **Problem:** Big bodies moving fast read as miniatures, and the one fully built weapon plays at fast-forward.
- **Evidence:**
  - 17 swings the 1.65 km Astrid through a ~30 s slew in about 1 s (26×).
  - 25 plays the M-1C's 6–9 s wake-up in about 1 s (7×). The roll, wake, firing and 32 s flight don't fit into 42 s of mission time anyway.
  - 24 is labelled 1:1 but spans the 32 s slug flight.
- **Fix:** Keep rotations at 3–4× or slower, cut out the middle, and let the clock jump.
  - **25:** the roll at 5× (3 s). Then the wake and first shots at 1:1 on a hull camera (5 s: lids, cells, shell, bore pulse, the 7 m recoil, the fins). Then 1.5 s on the rock flash, with the clock jumping 32 s.
  - **24:** the call, then the fins crawling in against the clock at about 8× (the race is the suspense), then the hit at 1:1.
  - **17 and 35:** only the last degrees of each slew, at 1:1.
  - **8 and 21:** 4× with a cut.

### 7. [MINOR] No screen-direction rule, and the sketches contradict the action (8, 11, 15, 16, 17, 21, 27)
- **Problem:** Nothing sets which way the fleet travels and fires on screen.
- **Evidence:** In the sketch library an unflipped bow points left. On that basis:
  - 8 and 21 put the plume at the bow.
  - 11 puts the corvettes that are "ahead" behind the stern.
  - 15's Canterbury faces away from a platform "off its bow".
  - 17's muzzle bloom and 27's lance glow sit at the stern.
  - 16 says "holed stern to bow", a leftover from the draft's braking destroyer. A ship moving bow-first is holed bow to stern.
- **Fix:**
  - Keep Maren and the enemy screen right; the LREF travels and fires left to right.
  - After 21, bows point left and plumes right while travel stays left to right, so the flip reads.
  - Allow enemy reverse angles (15, 31, 33, 34) only over an enemy foreground.
  - At the Lee, point the bow at the frigate's rock. On map C the two threats are about 138° apart, so the platform sits off the port quarter, the frigate off the port bow, and 25–27 share one axis.

### 8. [MINOR] Camera bodies and the sound rule (4, 7, 8, 11, 13, 17, 20, 21, 22, 29, 36)
- **Problem:** Several free cameras hear "through the hull", and "handheld" (7) has no operator in space.
- **Evidence:**
  - 8 and 21 orbit freely yet hear "drive roar through the hull".
  - In 17, "a punch through the hull" is heard from about 9 km away.
  - 11, 22, 29 and 36 hear ticks or cracks with no contact.
  - 13 has a hiss in vacuum.
  - In 20, the Wallfish's hull can't carry the Normandy's break-up.
- **Fix:** Tag every shot with a camera body: hull camera (rigid, shakes, and the only one that hears anything), drone (silent) or tracker (silent).
  - **7:** on the Infinity's hull, shaking with each launch.
  - **4:** riding the shield. Clamps bang, then silence as it parts; the Nauvoo is behind this camera, so it can't be in the frame.
  - **8, 17 and 21:** sub-bass.
  - **20:** the Normandy's channel dies to hiss, echoing 16 as the motif for a loss.
  - **36:** land the camera on the hull at the end, then fade the ticking in.

### 9. [MINOR] No lighting plan; the reference frames imply the wrong scale (3, 10, 22–25, 37)
- **Problem:** No sun direction is written anywhere.
- **Evidence:**
  - The reference frames for 10 and 37 show a daylit planet filling the frame from low orbit.
  - In the story, Maren is about 1.8° across at T+1:10 (about 70 px at 40 mm) and about 14° from map D's 54,000 km stand-off.
  - Map D's stand-off also conflicts with 37's "city lights below".
- **Fix:** Adopt what the board already implies (a crescent in 3, the night side in 37): the sun is behind Maren.
  - "In the lee" then reads as darkness, and the fins glow against black (the fins1dark look, 23–25).
  - Site 1 sits on the night side, so in 37 its lights can visibly go out, which pictures "Site One's dark."
  - Show stars only where a real exposure would, never behind a sunlit hull.

### 10. [MINOR] Narrow lens range and a repeated set-up (8/21, 10, 14, 37)
- **Problem:** Nearly everything is shot on normal lenses at medium sizes.
- **Evidence:** 29 of 35 lensed shots sit at 24–50 mm, with 4 CUs and no ECUs. 8 and 21 share a set-up: WS, 28 mm, a 90° orbit, the drive lighting.
- **Fix:**
  - Add 1–1.5 s ECUs the rig supports: CIWS spin-up (14), M-1C cells (25), a lens going violet (32).
  - Make 8 a long-lens shot of the whole group lighting up together; a 28 mm orbit can't show it. Make 21 a hull-camera flip.
  - **10:** arc to dead ahead as the fins extend, so they collapse to slivers and the move explains "edge-on".
  - **37:** frame end-on, so three glowing fins and a gap read as a broken cross. The fins take about 20 s at 1:1, so start them part-way out.

### 11. [MINOR] Dangling beats cost reading time (26, 28, 29, 31)
- **Problem:** Four beats never pay off on screen.
- **Evidence:** The Donnager's answer, the Excelsior's shield-park line, the Infinity heading home, and Breakwater's open missile cells.
- **Fix:** Cut them, or pay off the missile cells.

### 12. [NIT] Board housekeeping (3, 6, 12, 17, 18–21, 35)
- **Problem:** Several labels don't match what will be on screen.
- **Evidence:**
  - 6, 12, 17 and 35 contain internal cuts, and 17 and 35 claim "compressed 26×/30×" across a cut.
  - In 3, the VFX calls for 12 flashes and the action says 13 exits. The fleet list names 14 ships, while the line says "Thirteen for thirteen".
  - Shots 18–20 are still the Breakers fight but open Act III.
  - "The lee of the Lee" is hard to parse at reading speed.
- **Fix:**
  - List internal cuts as a/b shots, each with its own duration, clock reading and subtitle timing.
  - Make the ship counts agree.
  - Move the act break to 21.
  - Rename the rock in the dialogue.

## What works
- **The skeleton:** arrival, long reach, the Lee, then hammer and anvil. It has real setups and payoffs: the spinal cannon (17 → 35), Skerry's rounds (5 → 30), and the fins as a running thread (10, 11, 23, 24, 28, 36, 37).
- **Honest scale:**
  - **9:** at 1,200 mm, Skerry is a disc a quarter of the frame wide from about 260,000 km, and its flashes are a second old.
  - **11:** at 24 mm, a rock 20 km off crosses the 30 km-wide frame in about 2 s at true speed.
  - **22:** the boulder field under the Endeavor.
  - **29:** looking along the line from the rock's surface.
- **Sound:** most notes already follow the Expanse rule. 3's thuds as score, 33's "no air, no bangs", 35's drop-out and single boom, and 16's channel dying to hiss all work.
- **Time and continuity:** slow motion is saved for the kill (33, 34) against a compressed approach. Damage (fins, stump, scars, shield) is carried shot by shot.
