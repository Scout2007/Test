# Round 1 review: cinematography and editing

## Verdict
The four-act shape and beat order are right, and several shots sell scale honestly. As timed, though, the film can't be watched and read at once. Text is on screen about 80% of the time, 31 of 37 shots run 3–5 s whatever they carry, and at the stated lenses most kills are a few pixels wide. Re-time to about 3:20, cut the subtitles hard, add long-lens coverage and a clock/HUD plan, and set up the Canterbury, the Normandy and Breakwater before they matter.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MAJOR] Subtitles bury the picture (film-wide; worst: 28, 5, 32, 36)
- **Problem:** 36 of 37 shots carry comm lines, 51 in all. At 15 cps, with at least 1 s per line, they need about 132 of the 165 s. Viewers read instead of watching, and the setup (5), the geography (28) and the resolution (36) can't be read at all.
- **Evidence:**
  - Reading rates (text only): 28 runs at 29 cps (117 characters in 4 s, over HUD labels); 5 at 26; 32 at 25; 36 at 22; 6, 20 and 30 at 19; and eight more shots at 15–16.
  - Shot 17 fits three speakers and a cut into 5 s.
  - The tag EXTENUATING CIRCUMSTANCES (25 characters) appears six times.
- **Fix:**
  - Don't subtitle what the picture shows: drop 2's line and trim 11, 14, 21 and 25.
  - Cut four dangling beats that never pay off: the Donnager's answer (26), the Excelsior's park line (28), the Infinity going home (29) and Breakwater's open cells (31).
  - Tags: ACTUAL, EXTENUATING, ASTRID, ENDEAVOR, set small and dim. Keep lines off the cuts.
  - Rewrites, each 12 cps or less at the durations in #2:
    - **5:** "Skerry's thrown three rounds down our lane." / "They'll be hours." Label the HUD "ARRIVES T+6:40". From the T+0:04 launch, the sketch's "TOF 6 h 40 m" lands at T+6:44, not at shot 30's T+6:40.
    - **6:** "Picture's up. Breakwater's right where the brief said."
    - **9:** "Splash on Skerry. Their laser's gone." Viewers will take "two tracks down" to mean two of the three rounds.
    - **20:** "One's through—on Normandy!" Move "Normandy's gone. Close the gap." to 21, whose own line becomes "Turnover. One at a time."
    - **28 and 29:** "The Lee, Breakwater, Site One: one line. Spend wave on my mark." / "Mark." / "Spend wave away."
    - **30:** "Skerry's rounds. Right on time." / "All ships, left two degrees."
    - **32:** "Their net's down." / "All arrays: dazzle Breakwater."
    - **36:** "Actual, Maren's asking for terms." / "Site One goes dark first." "MDC" is never introduced.

### 2. [MAJOR] One tempo throughout starves the key beats (runtime)
- **Problem:** 31 of 37 shots run 3–5 s (mean 4.5 s), whatever they carry. There is no slow opening, no fast ambush and no breath after a death.
- **Evidence:** Shot 35 fits a slew, a shot, a three-minute flight and the impact into 7 s. The costly victory (36–37) gets 11 s, and the 2 h 10 min coast (10→11) is a plain cut.
- **Fix:**
  - Re-time:
    - 1 → 8 s (the ship needs about 3 s after the bloom); 3 → 7; 5+6 → 12 (#4).
    - 10 → 7 (play the coast on the clock); 16 → 5 (hold on the hiss); 17 → 8.
    - 24 → 6; 25 → 9; 28 → 7; 35 → 10; 36 → 7; 37 → 12 or more.
  - Cut the ambush (13–16) faster: 2–3 s shots with ECU inserts.
  - In 35, keep the flight. After "Impact in three minutes", hold on the crippled Breakwater, its turrets swinging uselessly and the clock racing, then cut to the hit. That hold is the film's best suspense.
  - About **3:20** in total, which the story needs. To stay under 3:00, fold 19 into 18 and 26 into 27, and drop the park business in 4.

### 3. [MAJOR] Real lenses can't see the far end of the exchanges (3, 15, 17, 24–27, 35)
- **Problem:** Kills happen at 40–15,000 km, but they're framed on 28–50 mm lenses set for the near ship.
- **Evidence (36 mm sensor):**
  - **27:** at 28 mm, a 350 m frigate 40 km away is about 15 px wide, so its break and pods can't be seen.
  - **26:** at 40 mm, the frigate is about 10 px.
  - **15:** at 50 mm, the Canterbury 600 km away is about 1 px.
  - **3:** Skerry is about 57° from Maren, and the 85 mm frame is only 24° wide.
- **Fix:**
  - Add a long-lens "tracker" camera: 600–2,000 mm, heavy slow pans, sensor noise.
  - **27:** a 2 s nose CU, then 1,000 mm on the frigate, which fills about a quarter of the frame.
  - **26:** 600 mm from the Endeavor's hull as the frigate clears its rock and fires, then the hull camera shakes with the hit.
  - Match framings so each hit reads as payback:
    - 17's impact shows 15's rock.
    - 24 gets a half-second glimpse of the platform, and 25's impact matches it.
    - 35's impact uses 34's angle, so the slug enters the Casaba breach.
  - Drop Skerry from 3.

### 4. [MAJOR] No clock or HUD plan (Acts II–IV)
- **Problem:** 32 of 36 cuts jump time, but the board doesn't say how the clock behaves. There are only three 3–4 s HUD inserts, and none at the ambush or the Lee, where threats come from places never shown.
- **Evidence:**
  - We never see the Astrid 8,000 km back (17), the platform's rock (800 km) or the frigate's rock (60 km).
  - The five largest jumps are all plain cuts: 8→9 (38 min), 10→11 (2 h 10 min), 21→22 (34 min), 29→30 (58 min), 36→37 (1 h 25 min).
  - Shot 5 (the Astrid's feed) comes before shot 6 shows the dish.
- **Fix:**
  - **Clock:** small and persistent, clear of the subtitles.
    - Start it on the flash in 1 (T+0:00:00).
    - Run it at the shot's rate: spinning seconds mean compressed, crawling ones slowed.
    - On jumps over 30 minutes, roll the digits or dissolve.
  - **HUD:** one master plot with Maren screen right, 5 s or more per insert, no more than 4 labels.
    - Show the clock and "ENDEAVOR SINK nn %" in a fixed place, so the heat builds toward 23.
    - **6:** the dish (3 s), then 5's feed (4 s), then the master plot (5 s: Breakwater, Site 1, Skerry, the Breakers, the approach line).
    - **17:** the Astrid's bearing line swinging onto the platform.
    - **22:** the Lee's shadow, with the two nearby rocks tagged "?".

### 5. [MAJOR] The losses and the antagonist aren't set up (6, 11, 15–20, 29–37)
- **Problem:** The Canterbury is never seen or heard before it dies, and the Normandy never speaks. Breakwater appears at 2:11 and is dead at 2:34. The missile waves blur together.
- **Evidence:**
  - 16's hiss and "Remember the Cant." land on a ship with no face.
  - Viewers will take 29's launch for 33's arrival.
  - The spend wave's arrival (T+6:01), which explains why the kill wave gets through, is never shown.
  - 36–37 never recall the lost ships.
- **Fix:**
  - **Canterbury:** an early shot and line, e.g. its dish unfolding in 6: "Net's up. Going quiet."
  - **Normandy:** give it 18's line.
  - **Breakwater:** a telescope image of it in 6, so 31 is a reveal.
  - **The waves:** say "spend wave" and "kill wave". Add 3 s at T+6:01 of the spend wave dying as a flicker over Maren's limb.
  - **The losses:** one callback in 36–37, such as the two dead channels on a last plot.

### 6. [MAJOR] Time compression turns kilometre-long hulls into models (17, 24, 25, 35)
- **Problem:** Big bodies moving fast read as miniatures, and the M-1C, the most developed asset, plays in fast-forward.
- **Evidence:**
  - 17 swings the 1.65 km Astrid through its ~30 s slew in about 1 s (26×).
  - 25 plays the M-1C's 6–9 s wake-up in about 1 s (7×), and the roll, wake, fire and 32 s flight exceed its 42 s of mission time anyway.
  - 24 is labelled 1:1 but spans the 32 s flight.
- **Fix:**
  - Keep rotations at 3–4× or slower, cut out the middle, and let the clock jump.
  - **25:** roll at 5× (3 s), then the wake and first shots at 1:1 on a hull camera (5 s), then the rock flash (1.5 s, with the clock jumping 32 s).
  - **24:** the call, then the fins crawling in against the clock at about 8×, then the hit at 1:1.
  - **17, 35:** only the last degrees of the slew, at 1:1.

### 7. [MINOR] No screen-direction rule; the sketches contradict the action (8, 11, 15–17, 21, 27)
- **Problem:** Nothing fixes which way the fleet travels and fires.
- **Evidence:** In the sketches:
  - 8 and 21 put the plume at the bow.
  - 11's corvettes, described as "ahead", trail the stern.
  - 15's Canterbury faces away from the platform that is supposed to be "off its bow".
  - 17's muzzle bloom and 27's lance glow sit at the stern.
  - Shot 16's "holed stern to bow" is left over from the draft. A ship flying bow-first is holed bow to stern.
- **Fix:**
  - Maren and the enemy stay screen right. The LREF travels and fires left to right. After 21, bows point left while travel stays left to right.
  - Use enemy reverses only over an enemy foreground.
  - At the Lee, point the bow at the frigate's rock. The threats are about 138° apart on map C, so the platform sits off the port quarter and the frigate off the port bow, and 25–27 share one axis.

### 8. [MINOR] Camera bodies and the sound rule (4, 7, 8, 11, 13, 17, 20–22, 29, 36)
- **Problem:** Free cameras hear "through the hull", and in 7 a "handheld" camera has no operator in space.
- **Evidence:**
  - 8 and 21 are free orbits, yet the sound notes call for hull roar.
  - In 17 the punch is heard from about 9 km away.
  - In 11, 22, 29 and 36 the camera hears ticks with nothing touching it, and 13 hisses in vacuum.
  - In 20, the Wallfish's hull can't carry the sound of the Normandy breaking up.
- **Fix:**
  - Tag each shot with a camera body: hull camera (shakes; the only body that hears), drone or tracker (both silent).
  - **7:** on the Infinity's hull, shaking with each launch.
  - **4:** rides the shield: the clamps bang, then silence. The Nauvoo is behind it, out of frame.
  - **8 and 17:** sub-bass. 21 keeps its roar only as the hull-camera flip in #10.
  - **20:** the Normandy's channel dies to hiss, echoing 16.
  - **36:** the camera lands on the hull before the ticking fades in.

### 9. [MINOR] No lighting plan; the reference frames imply the wrong scale (10, 22–25, 37)
- **Problem:** No sun direction is written anywhere.
- **Evidence:**
  - The frames for 10 and 37 show a daylit planet filling the frame.
  - Maren is about 1.8° across at T+1:10 (about 70 px at 40 mm) and about 14° at map D's 54,000 km, which also clashes with 37's "city lights below".
- **Fix:**
  - Adopt what the board implies (crescent in 3, night side in 37): the sun behind Maren.
  - "In the lee" then reads as darkness, and the fins glow against black (the fins1dark look).
  - Site 1 is on the night side, so in 37 its lights can visibly go out.
  - No stars behind sunlit hulls.

### 10. [MINOR] Narrow lens range and a repeated set-up (8/21, 10, 37)
- **Problem:** Nearly everything is normal-lens, medium-size coverage.
- **Evidence:** 29 of 35 lensed shots sit at 24–50 mm, with 4 CUs and no ECUs. 8 and 21 are the same set-up (WS 28 mm, a 90° orbit, the drive lights).
- **Fix:**
  - Add 1 s ECUs the rig supports (CIWS spin-up, M-1C cells, a lens going violet).
  - Make 8 a long-lens shot of the whole group lighting up, and 21 a hull-camera flip.
  - **10:** arc to dead ahead as the fins extend, so they collapse to slivers.
  - **37:** frame end-on, so three glowing fins and a gap read as a broken cross. The fins take about 20 s at 1:1, so start them part-way out.

### 11. [NIT] Board housekeeping (3, 6, 12, 17, 18–21, 35)
- **Problem:** Labels don't match the screen.
- **Evidence:**
  - 6, 12, 17 and 35 hide internal cuts.
  - Shot 3 gives 12 flashes, 13 exits and 14 ships, while its line says "Thirteen for thirteen".
  - 18–20 still belong to the Breakers fight.
  - "The lee of the Lee" is hard to read at speed.
- **Fix:**
  - Split internal cuts into a/b shots, each with its own duration, clock and subtitle.
  - Make the counts agree.
  - Move the act break to 21.
  - Rename the rock in the lines.

## What works
- **The skeleton pays off:** the spinal cannon (17 → 35), Skerry's rounds (5 → 30), and the fins as a running thread.
- **Honest scale:**
  - 9's 1,200 mm Skerry: a quarter-frame disc at about 260,000 km.
  - 11's rock crossing a 24 mm frame in about 2 s, at true speed.
  - The boulder field in 22 and the view along the line in 29.
- **Sound:** 3's thuds as score, 33's "no air, no bangs", 35's single boom and 16's dying channel all follow the Expanse rule.
- **Slow motion saved for the kill:** 33 and 34 are slowed against a compressed approach. Damage also carries from shot to shot.
