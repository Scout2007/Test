# Round 1 review: military doctrine and tactics

## Verdict

The skeleton is sound and doctrinal: Skerry first, a slow swept crossing, shelter and vent behind a rock, nose-on waves from the laser shadow, cripple then kill. But Act IV's timing breaks the LREF's own rules (the spend wave lands an hour before the kill wave; the Astrid ends up leading into the anvil), and the fleet parks unswept at the rock the defender would predict. The defender, sharp in the Breakers, goes passive for the last two hours. Six MAJOR issues, all fixable without cutting a shot.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MAJOR] The spend wave lands an hour before the kill wave (shots 28–33, PHASES)
- **Problem:** Wave two (spend) arrives at T+6:01, wave three (kill) at T+7:03.
  - The spend wave's arrival is never shown or assessed.
  - The ring "unmasks" 47 minutes after the wave meant to make it unmask.
  - Shot 33's "dying decoys" have no source.
  - Wave three's launcher, time and size are never stated.
- **Evidence:** O3/§5: the spend wave drains PD ammunition and heat and makes the defender reveal itself, so the kill wave lands in that window under the EW peak (O5). In 62 minutes a warpless monitor with a battleship reactor and big radiators cools, reloads its CIWS and re-masks.
- **Fix:**
  - Keep shot 29's launch from the lee, but fly wave two on a timed, slower profile so it arrives 60–120 s before wave three. The killers have Δv to spare. The "line" is the missiles' terminal leg, not the launch point, so the launch time is free.
  - Make shot 31 the spend wave's arrival: Breakwater's PD reveals itself, and the destroyer retargets wave three around it.
  - Spread shot 32's EW peak across both arrivals.
  - Put wave three's launcher, time and size on shot 28's plot.

### 2. [MAJOR] The Astrid's dash leaves it leading the fleet into the anvil (shot 35, map D)
- **Problem:** Map D turns the fleet over at T+7:05, and the Astrid, 8,000 km behind, keeps coasting at 20 km/s.
  - It draws level with the braking Endeavor at about T+7:27. When the slug hits it is in front, about 11,000 km from Breakwater, closing at 20 km/s, with no stated way to stop.
  - Stopping takes 20,400 km at 1 g (past Breakwater's orbit) or 6,800 km at 3 g (about 4,000 km from the wreck), stern-first toward the ring and Site 1, with the commander aboard.
  - Separately, 23 minutes pass between the spears (T+7:03) and the spinal (T+7:26): time for damage control.
- **Evidence:** §2: the Astrid "stays at the back of the line… closes only for the kill". §13: avoid "leading with the Astrid" and "braking straight down the threat axis". O10 and HD7 assume the kill follows the cripple. At 20 km/s the braking distance (20,400 km) exceeds the no-escape range (14,700 km), so no ship can coast to the firing point and stop short of it.
- **Fix: stop first, then strike.**
  - The fleet brakes from T+7:05 as now and stops by about T+7:40. The Astrid stops within 11,000 km of Breakwater, with the escorts 2,000–3,000 km ahead of it.
  - The Astrid flips bow-on (58 s). The waves arrive at about T+7:41–7:42, and it fires a minute after "drive is dark".
  - From rest the no-escape range is 11,016 km and the flight takes 184 s, so "Impact in three minutes" survives. Later clocks move about 17 minutes.
  - *Alternative:* coast at 10 km/s (no-escape range 12,850 km, same flight time, 5,100 km to brake), at the cost of about an hour.

### 3. [MAJOR] The Lee: the right idea, but an unswept, predictable anchorage (shots 21–27, map C)
- **Problem:** The fleet sits still for 36 minutes with fins out, a few km off the one big rock that shadows Site 1, at a time MDC could read from the first burn (Defence §9). The frigate waiting 60 km away proves MDC predicted it.
  - The LREF never sweeps the rock or its surroundings, and the defender never mines it.
  - The attacks come late and one at a time: fins out at T+5:12, the platform at T+5:21, the frigate at T+5:24.
  - From 800 km the salvo gives 32 s of warning against the fins' ~20 s retraction, so the fins should have made it in.
- **Evidence:** O8 and D6 (sweep ahead). A 1 Mt mine strips fins to 3.4 km (WN §7). A stationary ship is a platform's best case (broadside no-escape range 1,019 km). HO7: shoot fins first.
- **Fix:**
  - **Shot 22:** Corvettes and drones sweep the Lee. A drone sets off a mine found on its far side: the film's only glimpse of the minefields, and a reason for D2's spacing. The AVPSA maps the rocks within 1,000 km, and the Donnager shells them nearest-first (O4): "Clear inside three hundred, working outward."
  - **Shot 24:** The platform sits at about 400 km (16 s flight) on a rock still on the Donnager's list, and fires as the fins come out (about T+5:13). It sees the whole ship, not fins "sticking out of the shadow".
  - **Shot 26:** The frigate strikes in the same minute, so the platform's slugs and its own land together. It unmasks at about 45 km behind a moonlet: a captain who knows LREF kit would not close from 60 km to 40 km into lance range.

### 4. [MAJOR] Nobody hunts the last ECW destroyer; Breakwater's cells never fire (shots 16–35)
- **Problem:** After T+3:52 the Extenuating Circumstances is the only network ship. It carries the laser mesh, return to sender, the cut to the ring's links (shot 32) and the kill wave's datalinks. Nothing targets it for three and a half hours. Breakwater's cells open (shot 31) and are never used.
- **Evidence:** HO9 is the defender's first priority. Without the destroyer, O5 fails, and wave three depends on seekers that Site 1 can dazzle at 50,000 km in 0.25 s (WN §1). With no relief coming, a defender does not keep 64 missiles unfired (HD6).
- **Fix:**
  - **Shot 31:** Breakwater ripples all 64 at the Extenuating Circumstances during the braking burn. The Astrid's PD kills them; after shot 16 the destroyer rides under the Astrid's umbrella (D2, D10). Comm: "Sixty-four inbound, all on Extenuating."
  - **Shot 33:** Show the backup, the AVPSA guiding the missiles over direct laser links (§3).

### 5. [MAJOR] Site 1 falls silent while the fleet flies down its sky (shots 28–35)
- **Problem:** From T+5:45 the fleet coasts and brakes down Site 1's line, from 140,000 km to about 50,000 km. Site 1 is a 2 GW laser with no heat clock that the ROE forbid striking. The storyboard shows no dazzle, no burns and no answer.
- **Evidence:** HO5, and §4.2 by symmetry: lasers strip sensors, lenses and PD mounts. At 1–4 MW/m² (WN §1), Site 1 holes the AVPSA dish, the lenses and the CIWS in seconds. Q6 allows "geometry, weather, smoke and EW", and the final approach uses only geometry.
- **Fix:**
  - **Shot 30:** "Site One's on our lenses." / "Smoke forward." Lay co-moving smoke ahead and refresh it at the turnover and the Astrid's steady (§6, D8).
  - Keep the mast shutters down, and turn the AVPSA edge-on.
  - **Better:** time the approach to cloud over Site 1 (O9, HD10), with a line on shot 28's plot: "Front over Site One from T+6:30."

### 6. [MAJOR] Hedgehog magazines don't add up; the empty Infinity goes home alone (shots 7, 29, 33)
- **Problem:** The Infinity fires 150 of its ~700 missiles (A-26), yet is "dry". No reserve is stated. It is sent alone, with four CIWS, 300,000 km back through the Breakers that just killed two ships, to a park under attack (shot 28).
- **Evidence:** O11: hold about a third of the magazine. §11: the tender comes forward only after the defence breaks.
- **Fix:**
  - **Shot 29:** The Infinity and the Pillar of Autumn fire wave two, and the Infinity empties into it.
  - **Wave three:** The Galactica fires ~150–200 Casaba killers at the drive, sized to cripple (O10).
  - **Reserve:** The Pillar holds a third; add "RESERVE ~700" to shot 28's plot.
  - **Infinity's line:** "Infinity's dry. Tucking in under your umbrella." It goes home with the tender after terms.

### 7. [MINOR] Skerry is under-used, and the exit sits inside its laser envelope (shots 5–9)
- **Problem:**
  - Skerry throws three rounds, although it has 46 minutes of warning and three tracks firing every 10 minutes: up to ~18 rounds in the hour.
  - Its ~500 drones burn in the depot.
  - Its laser battery is silent for 64 minutes, although the park is only about 275,000 km away: inside the ~300,000 km fin-kill envelope that O1/§9 say the exit must stay outside.
- **Fix:**
  - **Shot 5:** "…and more every ten minutes." Shot 30 then shows several clouds, still side-stepped.
  - **Shot 9:** "The depot flushed before the hit." Those drones become shot 28's park raid (forty, not six: HO8 against D11).
  - **Skerry's laser:** Show it dazzling the AVPSA in shot 6, or put Skerry at least 60° off the exit's bearing.

### 8. [MINOR] Only the Excelsior guards the park (shots 4, 18, 28)
- **Problem and evidence:** D11 and §2 put the PD frigate *and a corvette* on the park.
- **Fix:** Leave the Tantive IV there. Give its shot 18 line to the Rocinante, and use "Excelsior and Tantive engaging" in shot 28.

### 9. [MINOR] Loose drones and missing defenders (shots 6, 16, 19, 31)
- **Canterbury's drones (shot 16):** They "drift, suddenly unled", against D10. The Extenuating Circumstances should pick them up.
- **Missing forces:** Two frigates, six corvettes and both drone-control craft never appear. Twenty-two linked drones need a nearby control craft (HD9), so locate and dazzle it in shot 19. Add "Two frigates holding behind the limb" (HO6) to shot 28's plot.
- **The ring (shot 31):** Its stations sit 30° (about 21,800 km) from Breakwater and cannot thicken its wall. Reword shot 31.
- **Head count (shot 3):** Fourteen ships arrive, but the VFX says 12 flashes and Actual says "Thirteen for thirteen".

### 10. [NIT] Rule citations and EMCON
- **Shot 30:** Cite HO3, D4 and O8, not O13; it is a side-step.
- **Shot 12:** Cite HO5, not D10.
- **Shot 17:** Cite O4, not O7.
- **Shot 21:** It cites O13 but brakes straight at Site 1. Offset the line or cite D8 only.
- **Shot 6:** Cite O2 for EMCON. Also, "goes radio-silent" at T+0:09 implies radio before that; write that the ships used direct laser links until the mesh was up.

### 11. [NIT] Terms
- Plausible (no relief; Breakwater was irreplaceable). To motivate a call within 16 minutes, have shot 36's plot show the Astrid slewing onto the next ring slot (fixed prey, O4).

## What works

- **Skerry first,** with a cheap nose-on wave from the exit: a fixed, airless laser site hit by the right weapon (O3, O9).
- **Speed and heat discipline in the crossing:** 20 km/s (O8), and fins out only edge-on with the threat on the ship's axis (D3).
- **The Breakers ambush is the defender at its best:** cold pods, and a platform firing inside its no-escape range at the net ship first (HO1, HO2, HO9). Hijacks are limited to linked drones (HD9). The Canterbury's loss is earned.
- **Stopping to vent refuses the defender's central bet** (HD4), and launching from the rock's shadow is O9 done right.
- **Both sides respect time of flight:** Breakwater's guns stay silent and the LREF stands off (HO1, HD7, O4). Cripple, then kill (O10), is in the right order.
- **"Site One goes dark first"** follows from the ROE: the LREF can't strike Site 1, so it makes that a term.
