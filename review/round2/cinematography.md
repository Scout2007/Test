# Round 2 review: cinematography and editing

## Verdict
Revision 2 fixes most of round 1: written film rules, a camera body on every shot, fast cutting in the fights, holds where the story needs them, trackers that see the kills, and setups for the losses and Breakwater. Two problems remain, both cheap to fix. The HUD text isn't counted, so the fire plan (37) and the cost callback (53) can't be read. And two payoffs happen off-screen: both spinal shots fire on their last frame, and Site 1's lights go out about 1 px wide. Fix those and 3:34 is earned.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MAJOR] HUD text isn't counted, so the fire plan and the cost callback can't be read (8, 27, 37, 53)
- **Problem:** The builder's reading-speed check counts only the subtitles. On the inserts, the labels are text as well, and they are read at the same moment as the lines.
- **Evidence:**
  - **37, the plan for Act IV:** the sketch has five labels, and the action adds "RESERVE ~700". That is about 113 characters, mostly two or three facts joined by "·". With the 54-character line it comes to about 33 cps in 5 s, against a 12 cps ceiling. "2 FRIGATES · LIMB" never pays off, and "FIRING LINE 15°" means nothing to an audience.
  - **8:** labels plus the line come to about 23 cps.
  - **53:** "CANTERBURY · NORMANDY — NO CARRIER" (34 characters) sits under two lines (58 characters) in 5 s, so the film's one callback to its losses loses out to the terms.
  - **27:** a geography insert held 3 s, against the board's own 5 s rule.
- **Fix:**
  - Count HUD labels in the check, not just the subtitles.
  - **37:** at 8 s, build the plot in two beats, each tied to one clause of the line:
    - first the two wave tracks from Anchor ("SPEND 7:52 · KILL 7:53");
    - then the Astrid's firing point (its only label: "ASTRID").
  - Cut "2 FRIGATES · LIMB", "RESERVE ~700" (shot 42 shows the Pillar's pods staying shut) and "FIRING LINE 15°".
  - **53:** play the terms exchange first, then hold "NO CARRIER" alone for 2 s (7 s in total).
  - **27:** 5 s.

### 2. [MAJOR] The climax trigger and the final payoff happen off-screen (21, 49, 54)
- **Problem:** By the board's own clock, both spinal shots fire on the last frame of their shot. The ending's key event is also too small to see at the stated lens.
- **Evidence:**
  - **21:** starts at T+3:52:36 and runs 4 s at 1:1. 22 is timed 108 s after T+3:52:40, so the shot fires on the cut.
  - **49:** runs from T+7:54:37 for 3 s and "fires at T+7:54:40", again on the cut. The bloom, the climax's hero effect, gets no frames.
  - **54:** at the stand-off (8,500 km from Breakwater, 25° off its zenith) the Endeavor is about 50,000 km from Maren's centre. There Maren is 14.7° across, about 520 px at 35 mm, or roughly 25 km per pixel. Site 1's lights are 1–2 px in a 9 s locked-off shot.
- **Fix:**
  - **49:** start at T+7:54:38, so it fires at T+7:54:40 with about 1 s of bloom on screen, and start 50 when the bloom ends.
  - **21:** fire about 2.5 s into the shot, and count 22's 108 s jump from that moment.
  - **54:** add a 2 s shot before the hold: a 2,000 mm tracker on Site 1's plateau as its lights go dark. At that lens a 5 km site is about 10 px. Take the 2 s from 54's 9.

### 3. [MINOR] Screen direction breaks in the Anchor exchange and on the Casaba jets (31, 33–34, 47–51)
- **Problem:** The Anchor axis is sound, but the sketches and the rule text disagree with it.
- **Evidence:**
  - After the turnover the bows point left, so the frigate "off the port bow" is screen **left** in the master shot. That contradicts "enemy screen right" without saying so.
  - **31:** the slug comes in from the upper left, the bow side, yet 30 put the platform's flash at the top right (the port quarter).
  - **33→34:** the lance leaves the nose toward screen left in 33, but in 34 it enters from the left and travels right.
  - **47:** the Casaba jets come in from screen right, so the kill wave appears to arrive from the enemy's side. 48, 50 and 51 inherit that framing.
- **Fix:**
  - Name the exception in the rule: at Anchor, the frigate is the one enemy on screen left.
  - Flip 31's slug so it comes from the right.
  - Either mirror 34, or shoot 33 and 34 looking along the line of fire, into depth.
  - Turn Breakwater in 47 so the jets come from screen left, and carry that angle through 50–51.

### 4. [MINOR] Tempo and text at the losses and the showpieces (17, 20, 25–26, 29, 35, 50, 52, 54)
- **Problem:** Some holds are filled with text, and some showpieces still carry lines that only repeat the picture.
- **Evidence:**
  - **20:** the hold on the hiss carries two lines (47 characters). Both have to follow the break-up, so they run at about 19 cps in the last ~2.5 s and leave no silence.
  - **25:** 3 s covers the dive, the bomb, the break-up and the hiss, and 26 opens with "Normandy's gone." There is no hold.
  - **Lines that repeat the picture:** 17 ("PD free. Chaff, smoke."), 29 (SINK 94 %, shown in 27 a moment earlier), 35 ("Batteries up. Fire.") and 52.
  - **35:** the 1:1 part is only 3 s, less than the M-1C's 6–9 s wake plus its 1.2 s charge.
  - **54:** the last line (52 characters, about 4.3 s) sits on the final image until the cut to black.
  - **50:** 4 s is brief for the film's suspense beat.
- **Fix:**
  - **20:** keep only "Canterbury's gone."; 24's NET DEGRADED tag already says the net is the Extenuating's.
  - **25:** 5 s, with 2 s on the hiss. Drop "Normandy's gone." from 26.
  - Cut the lines in 17, 29 and 35, so the countermeasures and the M-1C play with hull sound only.
  - **35:** 3 s of roll at 5×, then 5 s at 1:1.
  - **50:** 6 s.
  - **54:** "Site One's dark." early, the fins running out in silence, and the final line over black.

### 5. [MINOR] Framings that still undersell the losses and the payback (19–20, 25, 30→36, 45)
- **Problem:** Several trackers are set too short for what the shot has to show.
- **Evidence:**
  - **19–20:** a 200 m destroyer 40 km away at 600 mm fills about 8% of the frame (~160 px). The sketch shows it a third of the frame wide.
  - **25:** at 300 mm from 20 km, the Normandy is about 80–120 px.
  - **30→36:** the "glimpse" is a point flash on a ~15 px rock in a 35 mm frame, so 36's 1,200 mm close view of the rock matches nothing.
  - **45:** from the Astrid, Breakwater sits about 5° outside Maren's limb, but the 800 mm frame is only 2.6° wide. The limb can't be in shot, and the action's "on the plot" has no plot and no line, so the spend wave's meaning (it gave away their batteries) goes untold.
- **Fix:**
  - **19–20:** 1,500–2,000 mm.
  - **25:** 600–1,000 mm.
  - **30:** add a 12-frame 1,200 mm insert of the platform's flash, and match 36 to it.
  - **45:** about 250 mm, so the limb and the sparkle share the frame, plus one line, e.g. "Their batteries are lit. Steering round."

### 6. [MINOR] The checks pass, but they don't test enough (whole board)
- **Problem:** "All clear" covers less than it seems to.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Subtitle check:** it divides characters by the whole shot. It ignores tags, the HUD text (#1), a minimum time per line (44 fits two speakers into 3 s) and when a line is cued (20 and 31 must follow their events).
  - **Clock check:** it gives zero length to any rate it can't parse (12, 26, 35), so those shots go unchecked, and it can't see an event placed on a cut (#2).
  - **Missing rolls:** the jumps 10→11 (36 min), 26→27 (34 min) and 37→38 (60 min) have no roll or dissolve marked, although the rule requires one.
  - **Sound check:** it only matches "through the (hull|truss|backplate)". Every note complies on reading, but "roar" or "clang" phrasing would slip past. 47's "white noise" on a drone camera is ambiguous.
- **Fix:**
  - Subtitle check: add HUD text, at least 1 s per line and a cue offset.
  - Clock check: give compound rates an explicit end clock.
  - Mark a roll or dissolve on every jump of 30 minutes or more.
  - Sound check: test drone and tracker notes against allowed words (silence, score, sub-bass, channel, UI), and make 47 "the comm channels white out".

### 7. [MINOR] The heat readout never builds across Act IV (37–52)
- **Problem:** SINK values are given only at 27 (94 %), 37 (31 %) and 52 (98 %). Across the 34 s of approach, nothing shows the heat climbing, so the dump in 52 arrives unprepared. The rule doesn't say whether the corner readout is on every live-action shot, and a persistent readout would look like a game UI rather than a filmed spacecraft.
- **Evidence:** CLOCK_HUD gives the "fixed corner readout from Act III", but no shot between 37 and 52 sets a value.
- **Fix:** Show the readout only on inserts and hull-camera shots, and key its values, for example 39: 55 %, 44: 80 %, 52: 98 %.

### 8. [NIT] Labels (3, 49)
- **Problem:** Some camera labels don't match the rules or the frame.
- **Evidence:** 3 is a "tracker" at 85 mm, outside the tracker's 300–2,000 mm range. 49 is labelled EWS but "fills the frame". The first ACTUAL line (3) never says ACTUAL is the Astrid.
- **Fix:** Relabel 3 as a drone camera and 49 as MS, and tag the first line "ACTUAL · ASTRID".

## What works
- **Film rules:** the lighting, clock, direction and body rules give the layout artist a real plan, and the Act I–II and Act IV sketches follow it.
- **Setups and payoffs:** the Canterbury's "Net's up. Going quiet." (5) and the Normandy's line (23) give both losses a voice. Skerry's "ARRIVE T+6:40" (7) pays off at exactly T+6:40 on the clock (38). The 64-missile arc (40→43) and the named spend and kill waves also read.
- **The trackers:** the frigate at about 470 px (34), the 600 mm hull camera (32), the same-rock match 18→22 and the Casaba angle carried into 50–51.
- **The new shots:** 5, 16, 42, 43, 45 and 48 earn their place, and the hail's silence sets up the kill. 28 and 39 are procedural but carry a threat or a cost.
- **Sound:** every note fits its camera body, and the dying channel is now the motif for a loss.
