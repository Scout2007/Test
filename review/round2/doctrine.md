# Round 2 review: military doctrine and tactics

## Verdict

Revision 2 answers all six round 1 majors: four fully, two with a minor residue. Act IV now runs by the book. The fleet blinds the defence, lands the spend wave and then the kill wave 90 s later, cripples Breakwater, hails it, and fires the spinal from rest behind the escorts, with the reserve untouched. One major remains, and it is new: the hedgehog pack is left at Anchor unguarded, and a defender that predicted Anchor never goes after it.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MAJOR] The fire base is left alone, and the defender never touches it (shots 37, 41–42, FLEET)
- **Problem:** "Pack holds Anchor. The rest, with me." Three hedgehogs carrying ~1,950 missiles, the whole striking power of Act IV, sit at a rock for 1 h 44 min (T+5:43 → T+7:27). Their only defence is their own twelve CIWS, and the Astrid is ~100,000 km away.
  - The defender predicted Anchor: the mine, the platform and the frigate prove it. It can also see that no hedgehog left with the fleet.
  - Yet it does nothing to the pack. It spends its only Act IV missile salvo on the best-defended ship in the fleet.
- **Evidence:**
  - *LREF doctrine:* D2 keeps ships within ~10,000 km of the Astrid's arrays. The park, the other detached element, gets a PD frigate and a corvette (D11). A hedgehog's own defence is "4 CIWS" [Lore]. O11 says "spend missiles, keep ships".
  - *Detection:* The rock hides the pack from lasers, not from sensors. The picket buoys and the Breakers sensor posts can see the lee side.
  - *What the defender still holds:* about 250 cold drones in the Breakers, unfired pods with ~1 h of reach, and six picket corvettes whose listed job is to "chase stragglers" (Defence §5).
  - *Skerry's move:* HO3's answer to a big rock is to "ring it with canister clouds". Skerry to Anchor is ~282,000 km, 6.3 h at 12.5 km/s. Its last salvos (thrown T+0:34–0:54) could ring Anchor at T+6:50–7:10, while the pack waits. HD6 says to spend the cheap things; this is where they should go.
- **Fix (keeps every beat):**
  - Leave the Donnager (it has no Act IV role) and the Wallfish with the pack, and have the pack keep within ~50 km of the rock.
  - Shot 37's plot adds "PACK + DONNAGER, WALLFISH". Actual's line: "Pack holds Anchor with Donnager and Wallfish. The rest, with me."
  - Give the defender one try at the pack, beaten by the guard. In shot 38's window, one of the six nets rings Anchor instead of the lane (so "four more nets follow"), and drones wake on nearby rocks behind it. Comm: "DONNAGER: Clouds on Anchor, drones behind them. Pack's tucked in." The pack still fires on time.
  - Doctrine: extend D11 to "the park and any detached fire base".

### 2. [MINOR] Site 1 waits 72 minutes, and the fleet holds back a free dazzle (shot 39, PHASES)
- **Problem:** The fleet leaves Anchor's shadow at T+5:43, ~143,000 km from Site 1, but Site 1 first fires at T+6:55. The fleet then takes 55 minutes of burns before it dazzles at T+7:50, "since every laser shot would heat the sinks".
- **Evidence:**
  - HO5 says dazzle at any range and burn whatever shows. At 143,000 km, Site 1 burns a fin-class target such as a boom in ~40 s (WN §1).
  - LREF §4.2 says "Dazzle is free". A tracker needs only ~10 W/m², a fraction of a percent of an array's power, so its heat cost is negligible.
  - Dazzle counts as EW (§7), which the ROE allows (Q6). The board itself dazzles Site 1's trackers at T+7:50.
- **Fix:**
  - Site 1 opens fire as the fleet clears the shadow; the boom burn can stay at T+6:55.
  - The fleet answers at once with rolls, smoke and low-power dazzle: "Smoke forward. Dazzle their trackers, low power." Shot 44 then becomes full power on Breakwater plus the attack on the ring's links.
  - *Alternative:* explain the gap with weather, a front over Site 1 until T+6:55 that the LREF timed its departure to (O9, HD10). That would also put the ROE's "weather" on screen.

### 3. [MINOR] A 700-missile kill wave leaves the monitor "whole" (shots 42, 46–47)
- **Problem:** The Galactica's whole load (~700) goes in as the kill wave, yet "the surviving Casaba charges" only cut the drive.
- **Evidence:** Nose-on, Breakwater's lasers and CIWS stop ~30 missiles and Site 1 ~15 (WN §8). If even a third of 700 carry Casabas, ~200 jets land and leave the spinal nothing to do (O10). O11 treats the pack's missiles as currency.
- **Fix:** State the mix in shot 42 or on shot 37's plot, for example "~40 Casabas hidden among ~650 decoys and EW birds, all on the drive". The defence kills ~50 airframes, ~35 jets gut the drive section, and the hull holds. That is O10 as written, and cheaper.

### 4. [MINOR] The frigate at 45 km is credible only if the sweep's limits are on screen (shots 28, 29, 32–34, map C)
- **Problem:** The Donnager reports "Clear inside three hundred", then a frigate unmasks at 45 km, so the sweep looks sloppy.
  - Map C puts the frigate beside its moonlet rather than behind it.
  - From there it is in view of ships strung along a 300 km shadow line, and of the Endeavor's own radar, which sees a frigate at ~69,000 km (WN §5).
- **Evidence:** Pre-dug hides on surveyed rocks (Defence §2) and real cover close to a rock (§4.1) make a cold frigate in a cleft credible. A frigate beside a moonlet with open lines of sight to half the fleet is not.
- **Fix:**
  - Put it in a pre-dug cleft on the moonlet's far side (map C).
  - Make the Donnager's report honest: "Rocks inside three hundred shelled. Moonlets next."
  - Make the risk explicit in shot 29: "Sweep's not done." / "Can't wait. Fins out."
  - Use one range throughout (45 or 40 km). A captain in a fixed hide wouldn't close 5 km into lance range.

### 5. [NIT] Spacing, counts and small doctrine points
- **D2 spacing:** "The Astrid 20 km above" the Extenuating (shot 43) and the two destroyers "forty kilometres" apart (shot 19) are both inside D2's 50 km minimum. Use 50 km (the tracker still frames them), or write a close-escort exception into D2.
- **Shot 10:** "Fourteen drives" should be eleven: the Nauvoo, the Excelsior and the Tantive IV stay at the park.
- **The umbrella (shot 43):** The braking Astrid engages missiles that come up its drive axis, where its plume blocks its own PD. Cut or tilt the thrust for that minute (O13, off-axis braking).
- **The second turnover (T+7:16):** It happens off screen between the T+7:10 and T+7:20 nets. Note D8's conditions in PHASES: smoke up, one ship at a time, an EW peak.
- **The Endeavor's VLS:** I agree with holding them, but as the *fast reserve* (~4 min to Breakwater from 8,500 km, against the Pillar's 25 min), not for self-defence. Add "VLS 16 · 4 MIN" beside "RESERVE ~700".
- **O9:** At launch, Anchor is ~27° above the horizon of the neighbouring site it drifts toward. That site sees the waves' first ~10–15 minutes, almost nose-on. Its share is small, but add it to the WN §8 case.

## What works

- **Act IV runs by the book.** Dazzle at T+7:50, the spend wave at T+7:52:00, the kill wave at T+7:53:30, then the cripple and the hail. The spinal fires 70 s later, from rest and from behind the escorts, inside the no-escape range of a crippled monitor, with the reserve untouched.
- **The waves' geometry satisfies O9.** The launch is hidden, the waves come in nose-on to the only PD that matters, distance keeps Site 1 to ~15 kills, and misses clear Maren. Dropping the corridor was right.
- **The defender's timing is sharp where it acts.** The T+7:10 and T+7:20 nets bracket the second turnover, and Breakwater's 64 missiles arrive with the sixth net: HO1's braking window, used. One plot tag would let the audience see it.
- **Both sides show correct restraint.** Breakwater's turrets stay silent, and the frigates hold behind the limb (HO6). The escorts stop at 8,500 km, far outside every no-escape range (~1,200 km).
- **Anchor reads as competent risk-taking.** The sweep, the mine and the shelling outward; the platform just past the cleared ring; heat forcing the fins out; and the live threat, the frigate, killed before the spent platform.
- **The terms follow from the ROE and the firing line.**
