# Round 3 review: cinematography and editing

## Verdict
Revision 3 fixes every round 2 issue. The builder checks them, and I re-derived the timings and pixel sizes myself.

The user's five close-ups cut in where they sit and earn their seconds: the missile, the lenses, the M-1C waking and firing, and the seeker. *The flash* and *Site One goes dark* close the two payoffs that round 2 found off-screen.

What's left is minor:
- Act IV's run-up to the climax sags.
- *Birds away* points wave one at the wrong body.
- The board's own geometry puts Breakwater in Maren's shadow at its reveal.
- A few beats are a second short.

**No major issues.** 4:11 is earned through Act III. Act IV should give back 6–10 s.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MINOR] Act IV's run-up sags (42–51)
- **Problem:** The pace holds through Act III (53, 58 and 45 s for Acts I–III). Act IV, though, is 95 s, 38% of the film. Its first 45 s are ten beats before the kill wave arrives:
  - the plan (42);
  - the nets (43);
  - the try at the pack (44);
  - Site 1 scorching the boom (45);
  - the slow birds (46);
  - Breakwater's reveal (47);
  - the kill wave launched (48);
  - the umbrella (49);
  - the dazzle (50);
  - the spend wave (51).

  Each is its own small threat, resolved inside its own 3–6 s shot, and each carries text near the reading ceiling.
- **Evidence:** 42 and 44 are HUD inserts with one shot between them, and both read at 11.9–12.0 cps. 44 tells an action beat, the defender's try at the pack, entirely on a plot, and the pack has already "tucked in" by the time we see it. So there is no stake to feel.
- **Fix:**
  - Fold 44 into 46. The pack fires from its slot while the last drones die to the Donnager's guns in the background, with the line "Pack's tucked in. Slow birds away." That saves ~6 s and one insert, and gives the try a picture.
  - Trim 43 to 4 s: "Skerry's rounds, right on time." / "Left two degrees."
  - About 4:05 overall.

### 2. [MINOR] *Birds away* flies wave one at Maren instead of Skerry (10)
- **Problem:** The action and the sketch put "ahead, screen right, Maren's thin crescent" in front of the missile. Wave one is flying at Skerry, though, and from the exit Skerry is 57° away from Maren. An 85 mm frame is only 24° wide, so both can't be in shot. A first-time viewer will read wave one as heading for the planet.
- **Evidence:** In the builder's own geometry (the exit at −450,000 km, Skerry at 140°), the Maren–Skerry angle is 57.2°. At 85 mm, Skerry is a ~30 px grey disc and Maren a ~130 px crescent. A minute into a 30 g boost, the Infinity is ~530 km behind, about 4 px, so "a spark" is right.
- **Fix:** Put Skerry's grey disc ahead, screen right, and keep Maren out of the frame. The cinematic-licence camera is fine.

### 3. [MINOR] Breakwater's reveal happens inside Maren's shadow (47–50, 62)
- **Problem:** World E ("Breakwater over Maren's night side") lights all of shots 47–62 the same way. The board's own plane geometry puts the sun at 30° and Breakwater at 180° plus 15°/h from T+5:09. That puts Breakwater inside Maren's umbra from about T+6:35 to T+7:44. The escorts sit in it at their stop until about T+7:53.
- **Evidence:** At T+7:25, the reveal (47), Breakwater is ~3,000 km off the shadow axis, and the umbra is ~6,200 km wide in radius there. It comes into full sun only around T+7:45, just before the spend wave (51). *Blind it* (50, T+7:50) finds the Endeavor still in shadow.
- **Fix:** Choose one of two options.
  - **Keep the eclipse and light for it:**
    - **47:** Breakwater lit only by Maren's red atmospheric ring, the city glow below, and the plumes of its own 64 missiles. It's a strong reveal.
    - **49–50:** lenses and intercept flashes in darkness.
    - **51–58:** the climax becomes Breakwater's sunrise, under a low sun just past the limb.
  - **Tilt the sun instead:** put it ≥ 9° off the ring plane, and there is no eclipse.

  Either way, in 62 the sun sits ~15° from Maren's centre, inside the 35 mm frame's width. Keep it out of shot (the frame is only ±12° tall) and let the crescent clip, or the city lights won't read.

### 4. [MINOR] Three beats are a second short (40, 60–61)
- **Problem:** Each of these carries more than its length lets the audience see.
- **Evidence:**
  - **40, *Fire*:** labelled "ECU on the muzzles", but its sketch frames the barrels and the crest vent together, which is a CU. In 2 s it has to show the bore pulse, the blast, the 7 m recoil and "only the gun that fired vents". At an ECU on the muzzles, the recoil carries the muzzle out of frame and the vent is off-frame.
  - **60, *Terms*:** the callback "CANTERBURY · NORMANDY: NO CARRIER" (33 characters) holds alone for 2 s, about 16 cps in its window.
  - **61, *Site One goes dark*:** a ~10 px cluster in 2 s, straight after 60's similar 2,000 mm night-side frame. The viewer has to find the cluster, see it lit, and see it go out.
- **Fix:**
  - **40:** relabel it CU and make it 3 s, so the vent glow registers after the flash.
  - **60:** hold the callback 3 s.
  - **61:** make it 3 s. Centre the cluster on an otherwise dark plateau, with some surface texture so it reads as ground, not as wreck glitter.
  - These add ~3 s. The trims in #1 cover them.

### 5. [MINOR] The reading ceiling has become the target (8, 14, 30, 31, 34, 42–44, 50, 60)
- **Problem:** The load is down: 46 of 62 shots have lines, needing 41% of the film at 15 cps (48% in rev 2). But nine shots sit at 11.4–12.0 cps. Lines cued to an event read faster than the whole-shot figure the check uses.
- **Evidence:** 14, 44 and 50 are at 12.0 cps, and 42 at 11.9. In 34, "Port fin's gone. Cut it loose." has to follow the shatter, so it gets ~2 s: about 15 cps.
- **Fix:** Trim where it's free:
  - **14:** "No shields from here. Corvettes, sweep." (39 characters)
  - **30:** "Near rocks done. Moonlets next."
  - **34:** "Port fin's gone."
  - **43:** as in #1.
  - Aim for ≤ 10 cps wherever a line waits for an event.

### 6. [NIT] Check loopholes (builder)
- **Problem:** The sound check passes a whole note if it mentions "score", "channel" or "sub-bass" anywhere, so "RCS thumps; the score swells" on a drone camera would pass. "Hiss", "crack", "rumble", "pop" and "hum" aren't on the word list. The 5 s rule for geography inserts isn't coded, though every insert meets it.
- **Evidence:** The regex `HEARD` and the note-wide exemption in `check()`. Today's notes all comply on reading.
- **Fix:** Test the exemption per clause, add the missing words, and check that geography inserts run ≥ 5 s.

### 7. [NIT] *Seeker* focus (52)
- **Problem:** An ECU at 100 mm on the glowing cap can't hold Breakwater, at effectively infinite distance, in focus too.
- **Evidence:** Real lens behaviour; the shot calls for both.
- **Fix:** Rack focus from the cap to Breakwater as the seeker shutter opens. That turns the lens limit into the moment.

## What works
- **The M-1C sequence:** 38 → 39 → 40 → 41 is the film's best mechanical beat. The roll plays at 5×, the wake at 2× (so the 6–9 s wake reads as heavy machinery), the shot at 1:1, and the payback in the flash's own 1,200 mm framing, all toward screen right.
- ***Seeker* (52):** a real suspense insert right before the wall of fire. The cap burns, the shutter opens, and Breakwater grows from a point to a ~60 px sliver (at ~80 km/s, 2 s out is ~160 km), screen right as the rule says.
- **The other close-ups:** *Birds away* and *Lenses* add the hardware texture the user asked for without slowing the ambush. The 16–19 run (3, 1, 3 and 4 s) still cuts fast.
- ***The flash* (32):** 1 s, silent, then the call. It feels like an ambush, and it sets up 41's matched payback.
- **The loss beats:** the Canterbury at ~320 px and the Normandy with a real hold on the hiss.
- **The heat thread:** the SINK readout now climbs to the dump.
- **Direction and the clock:** the Anchor exchange reads on one axis with its exception named. Every jump of 30 minutes or more rolls, and the clock math behind both spinal shots holds.
