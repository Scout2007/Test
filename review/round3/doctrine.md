# Round 3 review: military doctrine and tactics

## Verdict

Revision 3 resolves four of my five round 2 issues fully and the fifth in part. The pack has a guard, and the defender makes a sharp try for it and fails for the right reasons. Site 1's duel now follows the doctrine, and the anchorage reads as a competent force taking a stated risk. One new major remains. The two-part launch is sound for the spend wave but not for the kill wave: it hands the defender the ~40 Casaba killers the decoys were meant to hide. It can be fixed by changing one clock and one line.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MAJOR] The kill wave's two-part launch unmasks its Casabas (shots 46, 48, PHASES, KEY_NUMBERS)
- **Problem:** The kill wave's ~300 decoy and EW multi-packs leave at T+7:08:30 on a 45-minute flight and arrive at ~45 km/s. Its ~40 Casaba killers leave 20 minutes later from a separate launch, boost at 30 g, and arrive about twice as fast. The board says they arrive "among" the decoys. In space they arrive in the same second, but the defender can tell the two groups apart.
- **Evidence, starting with what the defender learns:**
  - *At T+7:07–7:08:30:* ~780 slow tracks leave Anchor on straight lines. That gives the defender the waves' axis and both arrival times 45 minutes ahead. The defender knows LREF doctrine (Defence §1), and O3 says the spend wave lands 1–2 minutes before the kill wave. So it also knows which wave is which.
  - *At T+7:28:30:* a separate launch, with a different boost and twice the speed, marks exactly which ~40 tracks carry Casabas.
  - *Doctrine:* LREF §5 says "Decoys are missiles too" because each one the defender engages costs it a retarget, and §6 says decoys must mimic what they protect. Decoys that can be told apart by launch time and speed never get engaged.
  - *By WN §8's own numbers:* Breakwater (lasers nose-on ~10–20, CIWS ~7/s) and Site 1 (~15) make ~30–45 kills per wave. Spread across ~340 look-alike tracks, ~35 of 40 Casabas reach 2 km. Concentrated on 40 marked tracks, only ~10 do, so the cripple (O10) goes from robust to marginal.
- **The spend wave is fine.** Its slow birds are real small nuclear warheads that have to be answered, and a spend wave loses nothing by being seen early.
- **Fix, option (a), recommended:**
  - Launch each wave in one go, on the slow profile: the spend wave at T+7:07, and the whole kill wave, Casabas included, at T+7:08:30.
  - The killers have Δv to spare. Flying slow also leaves them more for terminal jinks and re-timing.
  - Shot 48 moves to T+7:08:30 and plays before *The anvil* (47). Breakwater's 64 cells at T+7:25 then read as its answer to ~900 inbound birds: HO9 against the ship that steers them.
  - Drop "Its last killers will follow at T+7:27" from shot 46.
- **Fix, option (b), keeps the clocks:** Make the decoys match the killers. Each Casaba killer's bus (A-26's MIRV buses) sheds decoys in its last minutes, giving ~40 real among ~200 look-alikes at the same speed. The slow multi-packs then act as the EW screen.

But Breakwater's lasers are already on this missile (the cap glows), and a PD laser kills a seeker at 1,000 km in ~0.1 s (WN §1). Open the shutter in the last second, or as the beam swings off.

Keep the Endeavor's nose on Site 1 so its fins stay edge-on (D3) until the terms are signed; the end-on framing already implies this, so say it.

Add the charts to the terms in shot 60 ("Site One goes dark first. Then the Breakers charts."), or soften the line.

- **Map E** still draws the hit at T+7:57:44 and prints that in its caption; revision 3's impact is at T+7:57:27.

## What works

- **The fire base is now doctrinal on both sides.** D11's new clause gives it a guard. The defender uses HO3's rock-ringing move with drones behind the clouds, from a salvo committed at T+0:24 on the anchorage it predicted. It fails because the guard and the tuck-in work, not because it is passive.
- **Anchor shows competent risk-taking.** The '?' rocks, shelling to 300 km, "Sweep's not done / Can't wait", a tracker watching the one rock that fires, and a frigate from a pre-dug cleft. Both sides look smart.
- **Site 1's duel follows O5 and D3.** The fleet dazzles at low power from the start and goes to full power for the blow. It smokes and rolls against the burn, and gives Site 1's dazzle to the Astrid, the ship with the bigger sink, once Breakwater dies.
- **The weapon close-ups fit the doctrine.** The missile's slow spin (A-25), the arrays' fast retarget, the M-1C's wake and single-gun vent, and a seeker kept shuttered through the run-in. Shot 32 turns the platform's one-shot unmasking (HO2) into a legible beat.
- **The kill runs by the book.** The Astrid fires from rest at 10,000 km, with ~17 s of margin inside the crippled monitor's escape time (O10, HD7). The hail comes first. The ~1,100-missile reserve and the 4-minute VLS reserve are explicit (O11).
