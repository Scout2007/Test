# Round 4 review: cinematography and editing

## Verdict
Revision 4 handles all seven of my round 3 issues. Nothing it changed breaks the film.

The new order in Act IV works as volley and answer: the pack fires (45), the kill wave leaves (46), and Breakwater answers (47). The cut from 45 to 46 holds, because the lens, the foreground and the clock all separate the two launches. Screen direction in the new *The pack fires* follows the rules.

What's left is small:
- Three lines are cued to an event and still outrun their window.
- The run-in to the climax cuts at one tempo.
- The heat readout lost its middle value when *Anchor ringed* was folded into *The pack fires*.

**No major issues.** 4:08 is earned.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MINOR] Cued lines still outrun their windows (45, 48, 60)
- **Problem:** The film rules now ask for ~10 cps where a line waits for an event. The builder measures each line over the whole shot, so it can't see this, and three shots break the rule.
- **Evidence:**
  - **48 *Umbrella*, 3 s:** "Sixty-four down. Thanks, Actual." (32 characters) can only come after the flashes stop short, in about the last 1.5 s. That is ~21 cps.
  - **60 *Site One goes dark*, 3 s:** "Site One: no power, no heat." (28) confirms the lights going out, block by block. It has ~1.5 s left, about 19 cps.
  - **45 *The pack fires*, 5 s:** the spend wave launches "on the clock", 3 s in. "Last drones down. Pack's clear." (31) has to fit between the drone kills and the launch, about 20 cps. "Spend wave away." then lands on the launch, so both lines sit over the shot's main action.
- **Fix:**
  - **48:** 4 s, or keep only "Sixty-four down."
  - **60:** "No power, no heat." (18), starting as the lights begin to go.
  - **45:** 6 s (the fight 0–3 s, the launch at 3–4 s, each line after its event), or trim the first line to "Drones down. Pack's clear."
  - Add an optional cue offset per line to the check, so the ~10 cps rule can be tested rather than just stated.

### 2. [MINOR] The run-in to the climax cuts at one tempo (47–53)
- **Problem:** From Breakwater's reveal to the Casaba jets, the shots run 4, 3, 4, 4, 4, 4 and 4 s. *Blind it* (49), *The spend wave* (50) and *Seeker* (51) are three different kinds of tension (our dazzle, their wall lighting up, one bird's last seconds), but they get the same weight. The approach doesn't accelerate into the slow-motion release of *Wall of fire* (52).
- **Evidence:** 49–53 are five 4 s shots in a row. The only change of pace before *The hail* is the slowed time inside 52–53.
- **Fix:** Cut 49 and 50 to 3 s each. Both already read at ≤ 10 cps with their current lines at 3 s: "Their net's down." / "Full power." plus SINK 88% is 36 characters, and 50's line is 40. Keep 51 at 4 s and end it on the rack focus. The run-in then tightens into the slow motion and gives back 2 s.

### 3. [NIT] The heat readout jumps from 31 % to 88 % (42–49)
- **Problem:** Folding *Anchor ringed* into 45 removed the only reading between the fire plan and *Blind it*. The heat thread now skips the approach, which is exactly where Site 1 and the low-power dazzle load the sinks.
- **Evidence:** The SINK values are 94 (29, 31), 31 (42), 88 (49) and 98 (58). The rule allows the readout only on inserts and hull cameras, and 43–48 have neither.
- **Fix:** Let *Site One* (44) carry "SINK 62%" as a comp tag, a stated exception, since it's the shot about the heat load. Or make 44 a hull camera on the Extenuating.

### 4. [NIT] Small leftovers
- **Problem, evidence and fixes:**
  - **59 *Terms*:** "the Astrid's firing line has already swung onto the next ring station" has no picture in a 2,000 mm frame of the wreck. Either show it on the last plot beside NO CARRIER, or drop it.
  - **46 *Kill wave away*:** open on the Pillar of Autumn's shut pods, sharp, then rack to the Galactica as its pods ripple. "One holds, one fires" is then seen, not just described.
  - **The sound check:** its suffixes don't catch "rumbling", "rumbled" or "whining". Clauses split only on ";" and ".", so "RCS thumps, the score swells" passes on a drone camera. I tested both against the builder's regex. The current notes all comply on reading.

## What works
- **The 45 → 46 → 47 cut.** The two back-to-back launches read as two waves, not one repeated.
  - The lens changes from a 24 mm drone among the pack to a 300 mm tracker from the Pillar.
  - The Pillar's shut pods fill the foreground as the reserve.
  - The clock jumps 90 s, and each wave is named in its line.
  - The spend wave reads as a dense spray of small plumes; the kill wave as ~340 uniform, heavier birds.
  - Our plumes stream screen right in 45–46, and Breakwater's 64 go screen left in 47, so the reveal plays as the answer.
  - Chronology forces this order now, and it's also the stronger cut.
- ***The pack fires* (45).** The defender's try now has a picture: Anchor as a ~180 px black disc ahead at 150 km, Skerry's clouds glittering wide of the pack, drones coming round the rock from the right into the guard's CIWS, then the launch. Direction holds throughout: the rock and the enemy screen right, the plumes and tracers going right.
- **The M-1C run (38–41, 12 s).** It follows the README's wake order at 2×, then fires in a CU that shows the charge, the pulse, the recoil and the single gun venting, and pays off in the flash's own 1,200 mm framing.
- **Reading and the clock.**
  - 45 of 61 shots have lines, needing 39% of the film at 15 cps.
  - Every geography insert holds ≥ 5 s.
  - All clocks run continuously, and the builder now checks the spinal impact (T+7:57:29) against the shot and Breakwater's state.
- **The ending.** It reads as a sequence: the water dump, the terms, the callback held alone for 3 s, Site 1's lights going out at 2,000 mm, then the broken cross against the night side, with the sun kept out of frame and the last line under the cut to black.
