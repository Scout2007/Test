# Round 4 review: military doctrine and tactics

## Verdict

Revision 4 fixes my round 3 major at its root: each wave leaves in one launch on one profile, and O3 now says so. Every round 3 nit also landed. **No major issues remain.** Three minor ones do, all about what the one-launch plan now rests on:
- what the spend wave actually carries and what lands (by the board's own numbers it can't "die" against the PD);
- decoys that must match the killers through the boost as well as in the coast;
- Skerry's clouds, which a defender with divert kits would have steered onto the pack.

None of them moves a beat.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MINOR] The spend wave can't "die against" Breakwater's point defence (shots 45, 50, 52, KEY_NUMBERS)
- **Problem:** Shot 50 shows "the spend wave dying against Breakwater's point defence", and 90 s later the kill wave meets a full "wall of fire".
  - By the board's own numbers the defence makes ~56 kills per wave at ~45 km/s. So ~514 of the spend wave's ~570 birds arrive, including ~81 of its ~90 nuclear killers.
  - If those are the contact-fuzed capital-ship killers the lore describes, Breakwater dies to the spend wave. The Casabas, the hail and the spinal would then be redundant.
  - The board never says what the spend wave carries or what lands.
- **Evidence:** LREF §5 says the spend wave is "decoys, EW missiles and kinetic kill clouds, with enough real warheads that the defender must answer". §4.4 sends bare thermonuclear warheads against "radiator farms, sensor fields". By WN §7, bare bursts strip fins and sensors out to 1–3 km, but breach a hull belt only within ~30–110 m. A 1 t bird that hits at 45 km/s also carries ~1 TJ, as much as the spinal slug.
- **Fix:**
  - State the mix and the fuzing: mostly decoy, EW and kill-cloud birds, programmed to pass wide (the ~49° approach keeps misses off Maren, O9); a few dozen real warheads set to burst at 1–3 km.
  - Shot 50 then shows standoff bursts among the intercepts. They strip sensors, radiators and PD mounts on the side facing the wave, which is O3's "spend" done literally.
  - Shot 52 meets a thinner wall. "The monitor is whole" stays true, because the belt holds.
  - The odds row becomes conservative: note the stripping, and that Site 1's ~28 kills are counted in full although the fleet has been dazzling its trackers since T+5:43.

### 2. [MINOR] The decoys must match the killers through the boost, and be steered with them (shots 46, 50, MSL-V, O3)
- **Problem:** At ~45 km/s the defence's ~56 kills exceed the 40 Casabas. If the defender can pick them out, it can kill them all, so the plan now rests entirely on the decoys. MSL-V's decoy "mimics a killer's signature and size with a deployable shroud", which covers the coast. The boost is where a defender with 45 minutes' warning, 30 m telescopes and the O3 playbook would sort them.
- **Evidence:**
  - One profile means boosting at the killers' 30 g (WN §3) for ~150 s over ~3,400 km. The birds leave Anchor's 18 km shadow within seconds, so most of the boost is in view of Site 1 and the neighbouring site.
  - At equal acceleration a heavier killer's plume is brighter by its mass ratio, unless the decoy's motor is sized to match.
  - The destroyer "steers the kill wave around" the batteries (50). If only the killers take the datalink turns, the turns mark them.
- **Fix:**
  - MSL-V: the decoy's motor matches a killer's thrust and plume through the boost, burning off-axis or carrying dead mass.
  - O3: "by launch, boost, speed or signature".
  - Shot 50: the destroyer "steers the whole wave round them".

### 3. [MINOR] Skerry's clouds are aimed at the rock, not at the pack (shot 45)
- **Problem:** The last salvo, "thrown six hours ago at Anchor itself", sweeps past "wide of the pack", which sits 150 km down the shadow. The pack has been there for over an hour, and the defender's drones went straight for it, so the defender knew. HO3's rounds are "corrected in flight by divert kits", and moving a cloud 150 km in the last hour takes ~40 m/s of their ~1 km/s. A defender that doesn't correct is being passive.
- **Evidence:** HO3; Defence §1 (the defender reads the LREF); the fleet's own answer to the nets (side-step as the divert burns show).
- **Fix:** In the last hour the clouds steer onto the pack's slot. The pack, having tracked them for six hours, side-steps a few tens of km as their burns show, then launches; the drones follow the clouds in and die to the guard. A clause in the action does it, and the comm can stay.
  - *Optional:* make the timing the defender's choice, too. The divert can shift the arrival by minutes, enough to catch the pods open.

### 4. [NIT] Show the backup for the waves' guidance (shots 47–48)
- **Problem:** *The anvil* is now framed as a strike on "the ship that will steer the birds", and it is the right strike. But the board never shows what happens to the waves if it succeeds.
- **Evidence:** LREF §3 lists direct ship-to-ship links as the net's backup. A-25 gives the missiles datalink updates. With the seekers shuttered until the last second (51), a wave without links would fly blind against a monitor that random-walks (HD7).
- **Fix:** Add a tag or half-line in shot 48, e.g. "BACKUP: ASTRID DIRECT", so the defender's best shot has a stake and a visible counter. I asked for this in round 1; it hasn't landed yet.

## What works

- **The one-launch plan holds against the 45-minute warning.** The defender learns the time, the axis and the count. But Breakwater can't outrun homing birds, its PD was already at readiness, and a spend wave that carries real threats does its job whether or not it is answered. The one thing worth doing with the time, going for the net, is what it does.
- **The anvil is the right answer.** HO9 names the target, and the net is the single point of failure for both waves. Timing the salvo to land in the braking burn is §9's window. The Astrid's answer, cutting its drive so its plume doesn't blind its own PD, is exactly O13's point.
- ***Kill wave away* before *The anvil*** gives Breakwater's salvo a visible cause, and the two launches read as one plan.
- **The pack's fire base is now sharp play on both sides.** It stands 150 km down the shadow instead of hugging the predicted rock, which beats HO3's ring by distance. The garrison's last drones follow the clouds in, and the right guns (the Wallfish and the CIWS, D6, D11) kill them.
- **The last minutes are now doctrinal.** Site 1 is verified dark before the fins come out, the nose stays on it (D3), the terms take the charts because the garrison has no override (HD9), and T-SEC is told it's next, not that it's clear.
