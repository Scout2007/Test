# Round 5 review: cinematography and editing

## Verdict
Revision 5 adds three strong ideas: the eclipse gives the kill its own look, the after action report frames the film, and the fleet traffic sounds like crews at work. The climax (*Seeker* to *Impact*) is intact and reads in the dark, and *The wait*'s sunrise is the best lighting payoff in the board.

The cost is reading. 179 of 418 s (43%) are text cards or plots, and the prologue runs 72 s before the first image. That is almost all 2D, retimable in the edit, so it doesn't block modelling. **No major issues.** Fix issue 1 first, and settle the 3D shots lengthened for dialogue before the render gate locks (1 h to spare).

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MINOR] The film has tipped into a reading film (prologue; *Skerry throws*, *The picture*, *Return to sender*, *Anchor*, *The fire plan*, *Sixty-four*, *Terms*)

- **Evidence:**
  - **The prologue:** six cards, ~1,050 characters at 14–15 cps. The user asked for "a quick intro"; the cold reader counted "about 15 new names in a minute".
  - **The plot inserts** grew from 31 s to 103 s. *The fire plan* (24 s) is the longest shot in the film.
  - **The 3D shots** are ~22 s longer, to carry lines: *The task group* 5 → 8 s, *Skerry burns* 4 → 7, *The net* 4 → 8, *Terms* 9 → 14.
  - **Lines that explain:** the cold reader flagged "the one laser site that covers her", "range on our radiators" and "eighteen klicks of rock". Crews looking at the same plot wouldn't say them.
- **Fix:** Cut the explaining, which also makes the traffic more real (the user's aim), and trim the holds:

### 2. [MINOR] Dark shots that need a key they don't have (*Heat*, *The anvil*, *Spinal*, *The wait*)
- **Problem:** Most shadow shots light themselves with lenses, tracers, plumes, the Casaba flash, and the city lights behind for silhouettes. Four don't quite.
- **Evidence:**
  - ***Heat*:** EN-DMG says the port-belt scorch "must read in shot Heat", but the shot now plays in the shadow, "lit faintly red". The scorch won't read, and the plume is a faint haze.
  - ***The anvil*:** the red ring and city glow light Breakwater's underside, but the camera is above it and sees the unlit top.
  - ***Spinal*:** 2 s end-on in the dark before the bloom, with nothing named to light "it settles".
  - ***The wait*:** with the sun rising behind the monitor, the hull is backlit, so "the light on the hull climbs from red to white" plays only on the rim.
- **Fix:**
  - ***Heat*:** add a hull work light at the dump valve, raking along the port belt. Request it on EN now, before the rebuild.
  - ***The anvil*:** either drop the camera level with or below the hull so the red underlight faces the lens, or state the silhouette look (ports and plumes as the key). Give the reveal 6 s: 2 s of dark shape and lit ports before the cells fire.
  - ***Spinal*:** name the RCS puffs and running lights as the key for the settle.
  - ***The wait*:** put the sunrise near the frame edge, so the hull takes a raking side light as it warms.

### 3. [MINOR] Held beats filled with text (*The hail*, *Terms*, *Sixty-four* → *Umbrella*)
- **Problem:** Three beats that should breathe don't get room.
- **Evidence:**
  - ***The hail*:** "Breakwater, Tidebreak. You're disabled. Surrender your ship." (60 characters) fills the whole 5 s at 12.0 cps, so "silence where the answer should be" never plays. *Spinal* then opens with "No response.", saying in words what the silence should say.
  - ***Terms*:** the exchange (135 characters) must finish before the 3 s NO CARRIER hold, which gives it 11 s: 12.3 cps. The builder can't see this, because the hold isn't a cue.
  - ***Umbrella*:** Breakwater's 64 missiles get a 14 s setup (*Sixty-four*) and a 3 s payoff.
- **Fix:**
  - ***The hail*:** make it 7 s, with 2 s of silence on the tracking turrets, and cut *Spinal*'s line to "Astrid, fire."
  - ***Terms*:** trim the first line to about 50 characters, e.g. "Actual, Maren requests terms. The AI's guns aren't theirs." Mark the hold as a cue.
  - ***Umbrella*:** 5 s, paid for by the trim to *Sixty-four*.

### 6. [NIT] One gap left in the cue check (*Payback*)

## What works
- **The eclipse.** The kill plays in the dark, lit by lenses, tracers, plumes and the Casaba flash. The sunrise inside *The wait*, then the impact in the first full sun, gives the climax a visual arc. The builder checks the sunrise stays inside *The wait*, and World E's lighting is specific enough to light.
- **The report frame.** The header, the numbered paragraphs, RESTRICTIONS on its own card and a SOURCES card that explains the clock read as an official document, and END OF REPORT closes it.
- **The fleet traffic.** It reacts: the time-of-flight exchange, "Vampires! Off the rocks!", "No beacons.", "Splash." Class tags come once, captions come on first sight, and the plots say ENDEAVOR HEAT.
- **The reading checks.** Groups over quick cuts, cued windows, first tags, captions and clause-level sound words now model how an audience reads.
- **The action runs.** The ambush, the lee fight with the M-1C run, and the climax keep their rhythm and screen direction.
