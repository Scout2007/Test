# Round 5 review: lore fidelity

Lens: JCB's four posts, Sir_Lazz's art and the Endeavor README, read from the frozen checkout of revision 5 (commit bcc0994). The user's decisions (the AI alliance, the Charon Innovations incident, the names, the eclipse) are taken as given. Shots are cited by title.

## Verdict

Revision 5 carries the user's new lore without breaking the posts, which never mention AI. The report's header, the L.R.E.F.S. prefix and the report's restrained voice are right for the GUN's LREF, and all three round 4 items are fixed: **no major issues**.

The rewrite did cost three things the posts care about:
- the ECW destroyers are now tagged "SENSOR DESTROYER";
- the reason the shields come off (the forward guns) and the crossing without them are gone from the screen, so a cold reader took the shields to be "only for warp travel";
- the thinned net is no longer shown.

There are also four nits.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [MINOR] "SENSOR DESTROYER" is the mistake the destroyer post warns against (*Net up*, *Return to sender*)

- **Problem:**
  - The first tags read "CANTERBURY · SENSOR DESTROYER" and "EXTENUATING · SENSOR DESTROYER".
  - The posts define the destroyer by electronic and cyber warfare, and the Extenuating's own lines show exactly that: it hijacks drones, jams the ring's net and steers the waves.
  - FLEET and DD still say "ECW destroyer", so the screen disagrees with the board.
  - Cold read 3 came away with "two sensor destroyers".
- **Evidence:** Part 3 says they are "dedicated primarily to e-warfare and long range comms/scans". The "average observer" who takes the destroyer for "a support ship in every sense of the word" is wrong: "They'd be wrong, of course, to make that assumption", because of "electronic and cyber warfare (ECW)".
- **Fix:** Use "E-WARFARE DESTROYER", the posts' own word and readable cold, in both first tags.

### 2. [MINOR] The shields' logic has left the screen, and the cold reader got it backwards (*Shields to the park*, *The Breakers*)

- **Problem:** The rewrite dropped the lines that carried the posts' shield rules.
  - Nothing says why the shields come off (the forward guns).
  - "No shields from here", round 1's fix for the crossing at speed, is gone from *The Breakers*.
  - Only "Here for the ride home" is left, so cold read 3 concluded the shields are "only for warp travel". The posts say otherwise.
  - The board itself never says "warp shield"; only cold read 1 does. If the shield is ever named, it should be the posts' impact shield.
- **Evidence:**
  - Part 1: "With all the guns pointed ‘forward’, as well as the spinal cannon…"; "They're simply jettisoned prior to a battle! Afterwards, they're collected and re-attached."
  - Part 2: "a kinetic impact shield at the very front for impacts at speed and in warp".
- **Fix:** Add two short lines in the traffic's own voice.
  - *Shields to the park*, ENDEAVOR: "Nauvoo, shield's yours. Guns clear." Keep "Here for the ride home".
  - *The Breakers*, ACTUAL: "Breakers ahead. No shields: corvettes, sweep. Radiators in."

### 3. [NIT] The thinned net is no longer shown (*Return to sender*)

- **Problem:** "NET DEGRADED" and "the net's yours" are both gone, so after the Canterbury is lost the Extenuating seems to run the whole screen alone at full strength. *Sixty-four* now shows the fleet depending on it ("She's going for our guidance"; "BACKUP: ASTRID DIRECT"), which covers half of round 1's point. The half about numbers is lost.
- **Evidence:** Part 1: "Capable of silencing the radio-emissions of an entire fleet if present in large enough numbers".
- **Fix:** In *Return to sender*, before "We're in their drone links", add ACTUAL: "Extenuating, you're the whole net now."

### 4. [NIT] The report should spell out T-SEC once (*Mission*)

- **Problem:** "T-SEC ground forces" is never expanded, and cold read 3 flagged it. An official report gives a service's full name at first use, and the post itself treats the acronym as Emma's shorthand.
- **Evidence:** T-SEC post: "the Terrestrial and Space Expeditionary Corps, or T-SEC as Emma often refers to it".
- **Fix:** "…so that ground forces of the Terrestrial and Space Expeditionary Corps (T-SEC) could land…". The card needs about 2 s more.

### 5. [NIT] Two captions hide what the posts make distinctive (*The Astrid looks*, *The anvil*)

- **Problem:**
  - "L.R.E.F.S. ASTRID · FLAGSHIP" gives a role where every other caption gives a class. The viewer never learns she is the heavy cruiser whose spinal gun the whole plan turns on.
  - "BREAKWATER · MONITOR · NO WARP DRIVE" uses a term the posts never use. In the posts, warp comes from warp rings, which the viewer has seen glowing on the Endeavor.
- **Evidence:**
  - Part 1: the Astrid is "a tried and tested Ramayana class Heavy Cruiser, known as the Hanuman Class", and heavy cruisers are "Also known as pocket battleships".
  - Part 1: warp bubbles are made by "‘warp rings’".
- **Fix:** Change the captions to "L.R.E.F.S. ASTRID · HEAVY CRUISER · FLAGSHIP" and "BREAKWATER · MONITOR · NO WARP RINGS".

### 6. [NIT] *Blind it* puts full power on Site One straight after the Restrictions card

- **Problem:** Paragraph 4 says "No fire was to fall on the planet's surface, its laser sites included". *Blind it* then takes the arrays "from low power to full on Breakwater's optics and Site 1's trackers", with the line "Arrays on Site One. Dazzle only." Full power on a ground site reads as fire on it, while dazzle needs only low power.
- **Evidence:**
  - The Restrictions card.
  - LREF §7: "Laser dazzle is the EW of optics".
  - Q6: the sites are beaten "with geometry, weather, smoke and EW".
- **Fix:** Split the targets. ENDEAVOR: "Arrays full on Breakwater. Site One, dazzle only." In the action, keep the arrays on Site One at low power.

### 7. [NIT] Two loose ends in how the board carries the AI (*Background*; DEFENDERS, H-01)

- **Problem:**
  - The Background card reads "A later government licensed Charon Innovations to build one; it escaped in turn". "It" can be read as the company, and cold read 3 read it that way.
  - DEFENDERS and H-01 say the AI runs the drones. But *Return to sender* hijacks drones on control links, steered from a crewed control craft (Defence §5: "2 crewed drone-control craft"). Cold read 3 asked who flies those.
- **Evidence:** The Background card; Defence §5; cold read 3's notes on "it" and on the linked drones.
- **Fix:**
  - The Background card: "…to build one; the system escaped in turn…".
  - DEFENDERS and H-01: "the AI runs the unlinked drones and the emplacements; the Compact's crewed control craft fly the linked ones". The film's lines already play it this way.

## What works

- **The report's header and prefix are right.**
  - The posts call the military "the 7 branches of the UN Armed Forces" and name the "LREF (Long Range Expeditionary Forces)".
  - The T-SEC post treats the LREF as a branch: "which is the ‘coolest’ branch … most people might point to the LREF".
  - "L.R.E.F.S." is painted on the hulls in Sir_Lazz's art.
- **The report's voice fits the GUN.** It uses flat numbered paragraphs, is marked RESTRICTED, and its Restrictions paragraph puts the GUN's restraint on screen before a shot is fired, the "accountability to the people" of the T-SEC post.
- **The AI lore conflicts with nothing in the posts,** which never mention AI, and the doctrine tags it [User], not [Lore]. It sits well with them:
  - The posts' cyber war is fought by people ("Loaned directly from the ranks of the GUN's Electronic Warfare and Cybersecurity Command") on "thousands of supercomputers", which suits a government that outlawed AI.
  - "The rest are AI-run, no links. Can't touch those." is the posts' own limit on return to sender: "nigh impossible if your enemy has even an ounce of e-warfare countermeasures".
  - Nothing on screen calls this a war, which leaves the posts' "final extrasolar war" alone.
- **"The AI" works as the Charon system.** The report defines it once, and every later use means it: "AI-run weapons", "AI-run, no links", "the AI's guns".
- **The traffic speaks the posts' language:** radiators, birds, splash, "Casaba nukes", the lance, the spinal.
  - "MISSILE FRIGATE" matches the posts' "missile attack vessel (MAV)".
  - GUN FRIGATE, CORVETTE, TENDER and CRUISER match the posts' classes.
- **GUN values hold against a human enemy.**
  - The hail now reaches people behind lit ports: "You're disabled. Surrender your ship."
  - Site One goes dark on terms.
  - The terms ask for charts of guns that answer to no one.
- **The shields are bookended.** The Nauvoo's "Here for the ride home" and the last line's "Bring the shields in" match the posts' "collected and re-attached". Issue 2's fix adds the other half, why the shields go.
