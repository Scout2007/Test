Operation Tidebreak

An LREF task group led by L.R.E.F.S. Astrid and L.R.E.F.S. Endeavor breaks the orbital defence of Maren, a world held by the Maren Compact. Nine real hours, told in 2:45 of film, every time jump marked with the mission clock. Built on the approved doctrines.

  • Runtime 2:45
  • Frames 3,960 @ 24 fps
  • Shots 37
  • Mission T+0:00 → T+9:10
  • Format 2.39:1 · 1920×804 · 24 fps · Cycles

Sketches are layout drawings; frames marked render are current Blender renders of the Endeavor used as look reference. Doctrine chips open the rule in the doctrine reading edition. Comm lines are GUN side only, as subtitles for now.
