# Agent 02 · Project digest

**Goal.** One compact file so the trailer writers don't each re-read the whole project.

**Read.**
- `HANDOFF.md`
- `miniseries/PICKS.md`
- `miniseries/options/BRIEF.md`
- for each of the nine episodes, only the picked option's section in `miniseries/options/epN_*.md`
- `miniseries/SERIES_PLAN.md` §1 (the concept, series rules and tag)

**Write** `trailer/work/context_digest.md`, under 1,800 words:
1. **The film in 5 lines.** Nothing that `TRAILER_BRIEF.md` §3 marks as a spoiler.
2. **The series rules** that bind the trailer: look, sound, no spoilers, speech, assets, and the tag.
3. **The nine locked episodes.** For each:
   - its tag (T−n), title and style;
   - its logline;
   - its 2–3 strongest shots (shot number, picture in one line, sound or line, seconds);
   - its best line, verbatim.
4. **The user's lore and preferences,** verbatim where they were quoted.
5. **Asset list.** The film's 3D asset codes from `options/BRIEF.md` §4.

**Reply** in at most 6 lines: the path and the word count.
