# Agent 03 · Research synthesis

**Goal.** Turn the accepted analyses into one format profile, and the questions the user must answer before any creative work begins.

**Read:**
- `trailer/work/research/ref_*.md`
- `audit.md`
- `trailer/refs/REFERENCES.md`
- the user's round 1 answers (in `trailer/STATE.md`)

**Write** `trailer/work/research/synthesis.md`:
1. **Format profile.** For each item, give the value, its source references and how firm it is (essential, majority or single source):
   - length;
   - act structure with target timecodes;
   - shot-length targets per act;
   - movement grammar (which moves, how fast, how motivated);
   - framing grammar (dominant shot sizes and lenses, composition habits);
   - transitions;
   - sound architecture (music sections, silence, sync);
   - voice and text approach;
   - the opening and the ending.
2. **Where the references agree, and where they conflict.** Give the options for each conflict.
3. **Devices worth borrowing**, ranked, each with how it would look in our world using 3D-only means.
4. **Angle candidates.** 4–6 distinct directions the trailer could take that fit the profile and are unlike the nine episodes. One line each, with the references behind it.
5. **Questions, round 2.** Number them. These are the follow-up questions for the user that research raised:
   - the conflicts to decide;
   - UNSEEN material to supply;
   - the devices to confirm;
   - the angle candidates to rank.
   Make each answerable in a line.

**Reply** in at most 6 lines: the profile in two lines, the conflict count and the number of round-2 questions.
