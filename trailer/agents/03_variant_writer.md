# Agent 03 · Variant writer (run once per variant)

**Goal.** Write one complete trailer variant in its assigned angle.

**Read.**
- `trailer/TRAILER_BRIEF.md`
- `trailer/RUBRIC.md`, so you know how you'll be judged
- `trailer/work/format_profile.md`
- `trailer/work/context_digest.md`

Open an episode file only to copy a line or shot exactly.

**Your angle.** The orchestrator assigns one of these. Each variant must stay distinct from the others in premise.

| Variant | Angle |
|---|---|
| A | **The countdown.** The T−9 … T−1 counter is the spine. Each episode gets one beat as the clock falls. |
| B | **One voice.** A single found-audio thread runs under the montage and ties the nine styles together, for example Ep 9's message home or a radio loop. It must be in-world and lifted or plausible, never a narrator explaining. |
| C | **The styles.** The trailer is about the nine formats colliding: grain, CCTV, advert gloss, a seeker feed. Each cut is a channel change, and the joke and the thrill are the switching. |
| D | **Wild card.** Your own angle, as long as it isn't A, B or C, fits the format profile and could carry the series. |

**Write** `trailer/work/variants/trailer_<X>.md` in the format of `TRAILER_BRIEF.md` §6.

**Rules.**
- Follow the format profile. Where you depart from it, say why under **Format match**.
- Speech: only what a real person in that medium would say, or a true trailer tagline. Lines lifted from episodes stay word for word.
- Pictures: the episodes' shots and the film's assets; new shots only with small additions. List every new render.
- No spoilers (TRAILER_BRIEF §3). The user's lore stays exact.

**Reply** in at most 6 lines: the path, the title, the runtime, the word count, new render hours, and the angle in one sentence.
