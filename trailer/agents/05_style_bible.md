# Agent 05 · Style bible

**Goal.** Design the trailer's own visual and sonic identity. It is drawn from the research and distinct from the nine episodes.

**Read.**
- `work/research/synthesis.md`
- `work/world_digest.md`
- `TRAILER_BRIEF.md`
- `RUBRIC.md` (lenses L1, L2, L4, L8)
- the user's answers, rounds 1 and 2

**Write** `trailer/work/style_bible.md`:
1. **Thesis.** One sentence on what the style is, and why it suits this film and these references.
2. **Image.**
   - aspect ratio and frame;
   - lens family and depth of field;
   - grain and texture;
   - colour script by act, with hex swatches;
   - lighting philosophy in space (one sun, eclipse, emissives);
   - how scale is sold.
3. **Camera.**
   - which camera bodies exist in-world (hull cameras, drones, long-lens trackers) or don't (a free "film" camera);
   - movement vocabulary with speed ranges and easing;
   - what is forbidden.
4. **Editing.** Shot-length targets per act (from the profile), transition vocabulary, and how cuts relate to sound.
5. **Sound.** Music architecture, the treatment of vacuum silence, voice and radio treatment, and the signature sound.
6. **Type and copy.** The type feel (name candidate Google or open fonts), card layout and motion, and the copy's voice, with 3 example cards.
7. **Distinctness check.** A table of the nine episodes' styles against this one, with what differs.
8. **Borrowed devices.** Each one with its source reference and how it is translated.

**Reply** in at most 5 lines: the thesis and the three strongest choices.
