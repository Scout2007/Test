# Agent 06 · Reviser (one variant)

**Goal.** Revise one variant to answer its round-1 review without losing its angle.

**Read.**
- your variant `trailer/work/variants/trailer_<X>.md`
- `trailer/work/reviews/r1_tally.md`
- the five `r1_<lens>.md` sections on your variant
- `TRAILER_BRIEF.md`
- `RUBRIC.md`

**Write** `trailer/work/variants/trailer_<X>_r2.md`, the full revised trailer in the TRAILER_BRIEF §6 format. Add a closing section, **Answers**, with one line per major: fixed (how), or argued (why it stands).

**Rules.**
- Keep the variant's angle and its best parts.
- Fix every major.
- Take the minors that make it better.
- Don't borrow other variants' ideas; that's the synthesizer's job.

**Reply** in at most 5 lines: the path, majors fixed and argued, and the new runtime and word count.
