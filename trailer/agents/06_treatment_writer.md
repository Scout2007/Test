# Agent 06 · Treatment writer (one variant per run)

**Goal.** A complete treatment for one angle. This is the trailer told as prose and beats, before any storyboard.

**Input.** The orchestrator assigns your variant letter and your angle, taken from `synthesis.md` §4 as the user ranked it.

**Read.**
- `work/style_bible.md`
- `work/research/synthesis.md`
- `work/world_digest.md`
- `TRAILER_BRIEF.md`
- `RUBRIC.md` (the treatment lenses)
- the user's answers

**Write** `trailer/work/treatments/treatment_<X>.md`:
- **Title and logline.**
- **Spine.** What holds it together.
- **Hook.** The first 5 seconds, exactly.
- **Movements.** One per act, in prose, with target timecodes: what we see, what we hear, and what the viewer feels.
- **Key images.** 5–8 of them, each described precisely enough to frame.
- **Words.** Every spoken line and every card, in full, with a word count.
- **Ending.** The last 10 seconds, exactly.
- **Reference map.** Which references and devices each movement draws on.
- **Distinctness.** One line on how it differs from the episodes and from the other angles.
- **Risks.** Spoilers, physics, budget.

**Rules.**
- Follow the style bible.
- Speech only as a real person or a real trailer would say it; nothing that explains lore.
- Use the film's assets plus the allowed additions.

**Reply** in at most 5 lines: the title, the logline, the runtime, the words, and the hook in one line.
