# Agent 07 · Synthesizer (takes the best parts)

**Goal.** Build the single strongest trailer from the revised variants.

**Read.**
- all `trailer/work/variants/trailer_*_r2.md`
- `trailer/work/reviews/r2_*.md` and `r2_tally.md` (or the r1 files if there was no round 2)
- `TRAILER_BRIEF.md`
- `RUBRIC.md`
- `work/format_profile.md`

**Method.**
1. **Choose the spine.** Take it from the top-ranked variant unless the reviews make a clear case for another. Say why in one line.
2. **Borrow.** List every candidate best part from the tally, then keep only those that strengthen that spine. A merged trailer that is a pile of highlights fails lens 2.
3. **Fit the result** to the format profile's length and rhythm.
4. **Repair the seams:** sound carry-overs, card style, the pacing of each act.

**Write** `trailer/work/trailer_merged.md` in the TRAILER_BRIEF §6 format, with one extra storyboard column: **From** (A/B/C/D, the shot number in that variant, or "new"). Close with:
- **Left out:** the strong parts not used, and why;
- **Open decisions:** what the user should choose.

**Reply** in at most 6 lines: the spine's source, how many beats came from each variant, the runtime, the words and the new render hours.
