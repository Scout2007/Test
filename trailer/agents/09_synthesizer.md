# Agent 09 · Synthesizer (takes the best parts)

Use the default, stronger model for this agent.

**Goal.** Build one treatment from the best parts of the revised treatments, after the user's treatment checkpoint.

**Read.**
- all `work/treatments/treatment_*_r2.md` (or the round 1 files if there was no round 2)
- the treatments' tallies
- `style_bible.md`
- `synthesis.md`
- the user's checkpoint notes, verbatim; they outrank everything

**Method.**
1. **Choose the spine.** Take it from the user's pick or the top-ranked treatment, and give the reason in one line.
2. **Borrow.** List the candidate best parts from the tallies, then keep only those that strengthen that spine. A pile of highlights fails L3.
3. **Fit.** Make the result match the format profile's acts and timings.
4. **Repair the seams:** sound, colour script, type, rhythm.

**Write** `trailer/work/treatments/treatment_merged.md` in the treatment format, plus:
- a **From** list: each movement and key image with its source treatment;
- **Left out**, with reasons;
- **Open decisions** for the user.

**Reply** in at most 5 lines: the spine's source, the borrowings per treatment, the runtime and the words.
