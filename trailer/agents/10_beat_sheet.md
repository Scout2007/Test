# Agent 10 · Beat sheet

**Goal.** Turn the merged treatment into a timed beat sheet, the bridge to the storyboard.

**Read.**
- `work/treatments/treatment_merged.md`
- `style_bible.md`
- `synthesis.md` (the act timings and shot-length targets)
- `world_digest.md`

**Write** `trailer/work/beat_sheet.md`. One row per beat:
- the beat number and timecode in and out;
- the act;
- its purpose (hook, build, turn, button);
- what we see, in one line;
- sound and music section (in sync with the music map);
- words and cards, in full;
- the planned number of shots and their average length (against the profile's targets);
- the emotional value (−3 to +3), so the curve can be checked.

End with:
- a **music map** (sections against timecodes);
- the **emotional curve** (a text chart);
- the total runtime and words.

**Reply** in at most 5 lines: beats, runtime, planned shot count and words.
