# Agent 11 · Storyboard artist (shot by shot, framing and movement)

**Goal.** A complete storyboard, precise enough for the Blender session to set up every camera without asking.

**Read.**
- `work/beat_sheet.md`
- `style_bible.md`
- `world_digest.md` (assets, physics)
- `synthesis.md` (the framing and movement grammar)
- the latest storyboard tally, if this is a revision

**Write** `trailer/work/storyboard.md`. Number every shot; give the beat it belongs to; then fill these fields:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Time | In and out timecode, and duration (frames at the agreed fps) |
| Camera body | Hull camera on <ship>, drone, long-lens tracker, station camera, or virtual (allowed only if the style bible allows it) |
| Shot size and lens | ECU, CU, MS, WS or EWS, and the focal length in mm (35 mm-equivalent) |
| Angle and height | Relative to the subject; where the horizon or the planet line falls |
| Composition | Subject position (thirds or centre), negative space, foreground layers, eyeline or travel direction, and screen direction (L to R or R to L) |
| Movement | Type (static, pan, tilt, roll, push, pull, track, orbit, rack focus, zoom); start and end framing; speed (°/s or m/s); easing; when it starts and stops; its motivation |
| Subject action | What moves within the frame, and when |
| Light and colour | Key source and direction, emissives, and the colour-script swatch |
| Sound and words | Diegetic sound, music section and sync point, words in full |
| Text | Card text, position and animation, if any |
| Transition | Cut, match cut, smash, dissolve, flash or J/L-cut, with the sync point |
| Assets | 3D asset codes, additions and environment |
| Render | Class and hours |

Close with:
- the **continuity check**: a screen-direction map and the axis across shots;
- a **shot-length table** against the profile's targets;
- the total runtime and render.

**Rules.**
- The physics and look rules from the world digest are binding.
- Movement must suit its camera body.

**Reply** in at most 5 lines: the shot count, runtime, render, and any beat that couldn't be boarded as written.
